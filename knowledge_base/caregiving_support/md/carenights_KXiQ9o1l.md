# CARENIGHTS

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/KXiQ9o1l*

Supports children aged 6 to 14 from vulnerable families with structured evening care services.

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Caregiver support, Free

## About this support
The CareNights programme is a structured evening care for children aged 6 to 14 from vulnerable families, including those referred through ComLink+ or other community partners. It addresses a critical gap in after-school support, particularly helping caregivers who are working, attending training, or managing caregiving responsibilities.

## Who is this for?
- Caregivers with children aged 6 to 14 and are experiencing caregiving challenges
- Low income (gross household income not exceeding $4,500 per month or per capita income not exceeding $1,125 per month) and/or ComLink+ families
- Families coping with extenuating circumstances beyond those described above will be admitted on a case-by-case basis

## How to apply?
- Referral through schools, social service agencies, and government agencies
- Submission of enquiries through Morning Star Community Services' Website

## What to expect?
- Structured evening care
- The agency provide safe and nuturing environment during evening hours.
- The agency aims to strengthen parent-child through facilited bonding activities and workshops.
