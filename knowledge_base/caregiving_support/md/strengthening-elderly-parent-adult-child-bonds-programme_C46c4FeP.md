# Strengthening Elderly Parent-Adult Child Bonds Programme

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/C46c4FeP*

A 3-year pilot initiative aimed at strengthening relationships between elderly parents and their adult children.

**Who it targets:** Seniors
**Citizenship:** PR, SG, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Families, Seniors

## About this support
This proposed programme will be implemented through activities targeting the elderly parent-adult child dyad to promote quality interactions to help the dyad navigate ageing transitions together. The programme will complement existing active ageing programmes funded by MOH and family bonding programmes funded by MSF.

## Who is this for?
Elderly parents and their adult children.

## How to apply?
Montfort Care will recruit eligible seniors and their adult children through their AACs.
