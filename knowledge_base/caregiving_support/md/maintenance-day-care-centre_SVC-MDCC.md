# Maintenance Day Care Centre

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SVC-MDCC*

Full day programme of engaging activities, exercises and personal care for seniors at day care centres.

**Who it targets:** Seniors
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Day Care, Referral required, Subsidies

## About this support
- Centre-based day care that supports seniors in ageing well in the community, as well providing support and respite for their family/caregivers
- Provides programmes and care support services for seniors at day care centres, such as:
  - Meals (e.g. lunch and tea break)
  - Basic nursing and health services
  - Suitable maintenance exercises that are created for seniors based on their Individualised Care Plan
  - Social and recreational activities (e.g. arts & crafts, games and karaoke sessions)
  - Personal care (e.g. bathing and getting dressed)
  - Mostly offered on weekdays, but some centres also provide weekend support

## Who is this for?
Seniors suitable for centre-based care setting during the day, and/or require supervision while their caregivers are at work.

## How to apply?
- You can apply:
  ·       Through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one’s condition and needs
  ·       At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)
  If you are interested to view the list of service providers, visit the [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Day-Care).
  For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline[1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or[ submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).
  - Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.
  - Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.

## What to expect?
Subsidies are available depending on the [means-test](https://aic.sg/care-services/home-medical#faq) result.

- Only applicable to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents
