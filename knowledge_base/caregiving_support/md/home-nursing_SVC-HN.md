# Home Nursing

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SVC-HN*

Nursing services provided at home for seniors who are unable to leave their homes.

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities, Seniors
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Home Care, Referral required

## About this support
Home visits by nurses to help seniors who are unable to leave their homes with:

- Vital signs monitoring (e.g. blood pressure and blood sugar levels)
- Caregiving training
- Care coordination
- Medication management
- Nursing care (e.g. injections, wound dressing and tube changes)

## Who is this for?
Homebound seniors and their caregivers who need help with nursing care at home.

## How to apply?
You can apply:

- Through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one’s condition and needs
- At any of the [AIC Links](https://aic.sg/Contact-Us)

If you are interested to view the list of service providers, visit the [AIC’s website](https://aic.sg/Care-Services/Home-Nursing).

For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline[1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or submit an enquiry.

- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.
- Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.

## What to expect?
Subsidies are available depending on the [means-test](https://aic.sg/care-services/home-medical#faq) result.

- Only applicable to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents
