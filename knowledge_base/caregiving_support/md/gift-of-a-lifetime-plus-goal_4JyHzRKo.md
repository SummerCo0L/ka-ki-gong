# Gift of A Lifetime Plus (GOAL+)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/4JyHzRKo*

The programme aims to provide eligible caregivers of Person with Disabilities (PWDs) with an initial capital of $5,000 to set up Trust accounts with Special Needs Trust Company (SNTC)

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities
**Citizenship:** SG, PR
**Keywords:** Subsidies

## About this support
The initiative aims to encourage caregivers from low-to-middle income households to proactively plan for longer term needs of PWDs through structured and accessible mechanisms by lowering the upfront costs required to establish Trust accounts and top up the trust account to receive the matching grant (up to $10,000) under GOAL+.

## Who is this for?
1. Applicants should be Singapore citizens or permanent residents of Singapore.
2. Applicants have per capita household income of not exceeding $3,600 or Annual value of property ≤$21,000 if PCHI is zero.
3. PWD has a Disability Verification Form (DVF).

## How to apply?
Applicants may request their social worker to refer them to SG Enable or they can contact SG Enable directly via phone or email.

## What to expect?
1. Screening will be carried out to determine Service User’s eligibility and suitability.
2. Case Managers will develop care plans tailored to meet the strengths, needs, abilities and preferences of the service users and/or their families with the families.
