# Severe Disability Assessment (SDA)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SDA*

Provides an assessment of your disability status conducted by a Ministry of Health (MOH)-accredited severe disability assessor.

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities
**Citizenship:** SG, PR
**Keywords:** Caregiver support, PWDs

## About this support
SDA is required if you are:

- Applying for the following long-term care severe disability financial assistance schemes administered by the Agency for Integrated Care (AIC):
  - CareShield Life
  - ElderShield
  - ElderFund
  - Interim Disability Assistance Programme for the Elderly (IDAPE)
  - MediSave Care
- Applying for supplement schemes of CareShield Life and ElderShield administered by private insurers – Great Eastern, Income and SingLife
- Undergoing a disability review to ascertain continued scheme eligibility
- Undergoing an underwriting to enroll into selected schemes

## Who is this for?
Anyone who require an SDA to determine your ability to perform each of the 6 Activities of Daily Living, as certified by an MOH-accredited severe disability assessor.

These 6 activities are:

- Feeding
- Dressing
- Toileting
- Washing
- Walking or moving around
- Transferring from bed to chair and vice versa

## How to apply?
To find a severe disability assessors near you, you may visit the [AIC's website](https://aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/Severe-Disability-Assessment) and search using a postal code, address, or your device's current location.

If a service provider listed informs you that they do not conduct SDA, please [reach out to AIC with their details](mailto:apply@aic.sg).

For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline[1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060).

- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.
- Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.

You can also visit the nearest [AIC Link](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)[ ](#_msocom_1)or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).

## What to expect?
- **Assessments are strictly by appointment only**. Please call ahead to make an appointment with the clinic or assessor before going to the clinic.
- You will need to bring along relevant medical reports or discharge summaries related to your assessment when you go for your SDA appointment.
- If your care recipient requires a mental capacity assessment to be completed to obtain certification for the purpose of applying for AIC schemes, please explicitly request for it when making the appointment. Do note that mental capacity assessments can only be completed by doctors.
- A typical SDA will last about 45 minutes or longer.
- SDA fees are as follows, if:
  - You visit an assessor’s clinic: $100
  - An assessor visits your house: $250
- You will be reimbursed the assessment fee which you have paid if you are assessed to be severely disabled.
- For CareShield Life claims, the assessment fee for the first disability assessment will be waived, regardless of whether you are assessed to be severely disabled, or whether the claim is successful.
