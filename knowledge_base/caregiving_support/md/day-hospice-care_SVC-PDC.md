# Day Hospice Care

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SVC-PDC*

A safe and supportive environment during the day for patients with life-limiting illnesses and their caregivers.

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities, Seniors
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Caregiver support, Referral required

## About this support
Provides specialised care and holistic support at centres during the day for patients and their caregivers, aimed at improving the patients’ quality of life. Services include, but are not limited to:

- Palliative care with medical and nursing support
- Emotional and social support for patients and caregivers
- Multidisciplinary team of doctors, nurses, medical social workers, art therapist, and spiritual counsellor etc
- Transportation for patients to and from their homes
- Engaging games and activities

## Who is this for?
- Patients who have been diagnosed by a doctor to have a life-limiting illness and a prognosis of less than 1 year
- Do not require 24-hour nursing assistance

## How to apply?
You can apply:

- Through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one’s condition and needs
- At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)

If you are interested to view the list of service providers, visit the [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Home-Medical)[.](#_msocom_2)

For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline[1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).

- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.
- Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.

## What to expect?
- Subsidies are available depending on the [means-test](https://aic.sg/care-services/home-medical#faq) result.
  - Only applicable to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents
