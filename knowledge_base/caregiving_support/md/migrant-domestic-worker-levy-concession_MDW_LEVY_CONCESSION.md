# Migrant Domestic Worker Levy Concession

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/MDW_LEVY_CONCESSION*

Provides concessionary levy at $60 per month if there is an eligible person residing in the household.

## Highlights
- Concessionary rate of $60 per month, compared to standard levy of $300 for 1st MDW and $450 for subsequent MDWs
- Each household is eligible for one MDW levy concession per eligible person, up to two MDW levy concessions per household

**Who it targets:** Families, Persons with disabilities, Seniors
**Keywords:** Caregiving expenses, Subsidies

## About this support
- Pay a lower levy of $60 per month if you live with an eligible person
- Levy concession can only be granted for one MDW per eligible person and is capped at two levy concessions per household

## Who is this for?
If you live with any of the following eligible person who is a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident:

- Child below 16 years old
- Elderly person who is at least 67 years old
- Person with disabilities (PWD) who must be certified by Singapore-registered doctor or an approved healthcare professional to permanently require some assistance with at least 1 of the 6 Activities of Daily Living (ADLs):
  - Washing
  - Dressing
  - Feeding
  - Toileting
  - Walking or moving around
  - Transferring

## How to apply?
Before hiring an MDW, you are required to obtain an approval from the [Ministry of Manpower (MOM)](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/employer-requirements) to hire an MDW.

If the eligible person is a PWD, you will also need to apply for the scheme:

- Online at the [AIC eServices for Financing Schemes (eFASS)](https://efinance.aic.sg/)

- At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)

No application for the scheme is further needed if:

- You or your spouse is at least 67 years old
- Your child is below 16 years old

The levy concession will automatically start once you meet the eligibility criteria and complete the required steps listed on [MOM’s website](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/foreign-domestic-worker-levy/levy-concession).
