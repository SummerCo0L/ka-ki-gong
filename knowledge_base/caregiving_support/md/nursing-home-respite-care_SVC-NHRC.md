# Nursing Home Respite Care

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SVC-NHRC*

Short-term residential care option to provide respite for caregivers of seniors with high care needs.

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities, Seniors
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Caregiver support, Referral required

## About this support
Provides short-term subsidised residential care at select nursing homes as an option for caregivers who:

- Need a short, full-time break for personal or medical reasons
- Need help as their domestic helper goes on home leave
- Require a break at night to rest, as their loved one has challenging night-time behaviour

## Who is this for?
- Seniors with stable medical conditions who need help with daily nursing care needs
- Caregivers who need a respite or are going away temporarily

## How to apply?
You can apply:

- Through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one’s condition and needs
- At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)
  If you would like to view the list of service providers, visit the [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Nursing-Home-Respite-Care).
  For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline[1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).
  - Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.
  - Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.

## What to expect?
Subsidies are available depending on the [means-test](https://aic.sg/care-services/home-medical#faq) result.

- Only applicable to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents
