# grovve

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/34sPdG1b*

An integrated, youth-centric wellness centre offering a safe space where young people aged 12 to 25 can seek mental health support.

**Who it targets:** Vulnerable
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Resources and Support, Social-emotional Support, Students

## About this support
- A welcoming space where youths aged 12 to 25 can hang out and access mental health support.
- An on-site concierge manages basic support needs for youths and their caregivers.
- For those requiring more in-depth support such as counselling or case management, a range of services are available through on-site partner organisations.
- Scheduled activities are also organised within the space to engage youths and caregivers.

## Who is this for?
Youths aged 12 to 25 and their caregivers

## How to apply?
Walk in during opening hours or get in touch with grovve via WhatsApp at https://mindline.sg/grovve/about/visit.

## What to expect?
Youths can drop by Grovve anytime during opening hours and speak to a concierge staff member.

- Staff will take time to get to know each youth better, whether through conversation or questionnaires, to understand how best to support them.
- Where further support is needed, youths and their caregivers may be referred to the relevant on-site partner organisations.
