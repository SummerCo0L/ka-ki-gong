# Healthy with KIDSTART

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/zrAAc6BN*

Support lower income families with Singapore Citizen children aged up to 6 years old with fresh produce packs.

**Who it targets:** Vulnerable
**Citizenship:** SG
**Keywords:** Free

## About this support
Healthy with KidSTART was designed intentially to plug the intersection of early childhood nutritional needs and to support families with doorstep delivery to alleviate the logistical stress. Fresh produce packs will be delivered monthly to onboard KidSTART families to:

- Introduce to parents the importance of early childhood nutrition and impact their desire to establish good and healthy eating habits in their children from young.
- Seed the appreciation of a variety of healthy produce early for children and encourage future healthy eating habits.
- Improve accessibility of fresh produce for onboarded families of fresh produce for onboarded families.

## Who is this for?
- Singapore Citizen children aged up to 6 years old
- Low-income household (i.e., household income of <= $2,500 or per capital monthly income of <=$650)The Healthy with KidSTART programme is for KidSTART families only. If interested in the programme, please enquire with  KidSTART practitioners on how you can sign up for the programme.

## How to apply?
The Healthy with KidSTART programme is for KidSTART families only. If interested in the programme, please enquire with KidSTART practitioners on how you can sign up for the programme.

## What to expect?
- Fresh produce packs filled with fresh fruits and vegetables
- Recipe cards featuring nutritious dishes that are easy to prepare for the entire family
- Invitation to cooking demonstration workshops by celebrity chefs and events
- Healthy plate that guides you to prepare better portioned meals for your children
