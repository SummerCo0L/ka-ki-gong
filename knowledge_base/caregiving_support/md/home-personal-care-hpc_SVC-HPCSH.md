# Home Personal Care (HPC)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SVC-HPCSH*

Supports frail or homebound seniors who require help with activities of daily living and/or housekeeping.

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities, Seniors
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Home Care, Referral required

## About this support
Provided by trained care professionals to assist seniors and their caregivers with:

- Activities of Daily Living (e.g. showering, light housekeeping)
- Medication
- Mind stimulating activities
- Elder-sitting and respite for caregivers
- Light household chores (e.g. sweeping floors, cleaning fans and windows, and washing toilets)

**Note**: From 1 April 2026, HPC will be enhanced to provide more tailored and frequent support for clients, including those with higher care needs. On top of the ADLs and IADLs support, enhanced HPC also provides new services such as technology-enabled monitoring to assure caregivers of the client’s safety at home. Refer to the [link](https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/o0cdiebU/enhanced-home-personal-care-hpc) for more information regarding enhanced Home Personal Care.

## Who is this for?
Senior who:

- Requires personal services to continue living well at home
- Is a Singapore Citizen (SC), Permanent Resident (PR) or foreigner

Caregiver who:

- Needs support in their caregiving duties
- Is a Singapore Citizen (SC), Permanent Resident (PR) or foreigner

## How to apply?
- You will need to get a referral from a hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved ones’ condition and needs.
- You may also contact the service provider for a discussion. Visit [AIC’s website](https://aic.sg/Care-Services/Home-Personal-Care) to search for service providers by** **postal code, address or your device’s current location.
- For further assistance, contact the Agency for Integrated Care (AIC) at [1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060), email [enquiries@aic.sg](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg) or walk in to a nearby [AIC Link](https://aic.sg/Contact-Us).

## What to expect?
- Subsidies are available depending on the [means-test](https://aic.sg/care-services/home-personal-care#faq) result.
  - Only applicable to SC and PR
- Applicants for care services may need to fulfil the services’ eligibility criteria and providers’ service boundaries, if any, and enrolments are subject to availability of vacancies.
