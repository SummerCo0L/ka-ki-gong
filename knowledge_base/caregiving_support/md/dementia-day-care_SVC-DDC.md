# Dementia Day Care

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SVC-DDC*

Mind stimulating activities, exercises and personal care at day care centres for seniors with dementia.

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities, Seniors
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Day Care, Referral required, Subsidies

## About this support
Services at day care centres help seniors with mild to advanced dementia continue living in the community and delay the need for institutionalisation. They include:

- Group and individual activities to slow down deterioration of physical and mental functions (based on their abilities)
- Individualised care plans for seniors with suitable maintenance exercises
- Assistance with daily personal care
- Caregiver support activities (e.g. support groups, talks and care demonstrations, etc)
- Offered on weekdays, but some centres also provide weekend support

## Who is this for?
Senior diagnosed with mild to advanced dementia, and/or require supervision during the day while their caregivers are at work.

## How to apply?
You can apply:

- Through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one’s condition and needs
- At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)

If you are interested to view the list of service providers, visit the [AIC’s website](https://aic.sg/Care-Services/Day-Care).

For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline[1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).

- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.
- Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.

## What to expect?
Subsidies are available depending on the [means-test](https://aic.sg/care-services/home-medical#faq) result.

- Only applicable to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents
