# Life! Mentors

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/ngaAPNbH*

A 5-8 year mentoring programme that supports children (6-13 years old) a fair chance to succeed in life.

**Who it targets:** Low-income
**Citizenship:** SG, PR
**Keywords:** Education, Mentoring

## About this support
The programme core services includes

- 1-1 mentoring
- Group mentoring
- Family bonding activities

## Who is this for?
Singapore citizens/ Singapore PRs aged 6 – 13 years old from vulnerable backgrounds (e.g. ComLink+ families, parental incarceration, referral from school counsellors).

## How to apply?
Walk in to any of  Life Community Services Society centres or referral by school counsellors or fill in the online contact form (https://life-community.org/contact-us/)

## What to expect?
Programme staff will conduct intake and needs assessment within one month of the referral. For higher needs and urgent cases, it will be conducted within two weeks. Programme staff will contact the caregiver via phone call or text messages to introduce the programme, share the online intake form, and arrange a home visit.
