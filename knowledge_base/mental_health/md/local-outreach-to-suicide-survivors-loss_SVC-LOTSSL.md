# Local Outreach to Suicide Survivors (LOSS)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SVC-LOTSSL*

LOSS provides immediate and long-term emotional support to persons who have lost a loved one or someone they knew to suicide ("suicide survivors"), primarily through support groups, texts, phone calls, and counselling sessions.

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Free

## About this support
LOSS serves persons grieving a suicide loss, who include self-referrals, referrals from police, healthcare, and community partners, and concerned third parties.

It comprises:

- Support groups for persons grieving a suicide loss, facilitated by SOS grief counselling specialists and trained volunteers with lived experience ("Healing Companions").
- Additional emotional support through phone calls, text, and counselling sessions (where appropriate).

## Who is this for?
Persons grieving a suicide loss

## How to apply?
Reach out to SOS via the 24/7 Hotline: 1767; or CareText: WhatsApp 91511767.

## What to expect?
- When you reach out to SOS via call, text or email, SOS staff or trained volunteers will provide emotional support and if needed, encourage you to come for grief counselling and attend support groups subsequently for longer term support.
