# Psychiatric Rehabilitation and Re-integration

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/Ic7eUpmo*

Long-term support for persons with stable, chronic mental health conditions who are in recovery.

**Who it targets:** Seniors
**Citizenship:** SG, PR
**Keywords:** Seniors, Social-emotional Support, Suicide Intervention

## About this support
Provides long-term support for persons in recovery to improve their quality of life and live in the community, such as:

- Life skills training
- Social and emotional rehabilitation
- Recovery support (e.g. symptom management, follow-up appointments etc)
- Psychological support (e.g. counselling, family engagement etc)
- Assistance with job skills training and placements
- Finding housing services

## Who is this for?
Anyone who is diagnosed by a doctor to have a stable and long-term mental health condition.

## How to apply?
- You can apply through the doctor you are currently receiving care from, as you will need to be assessed for the services required.
- If you are interested to view a list of providers, visit the [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Psychiatric-Rehabilitation-and-Re-Integration).

For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline[1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).

- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.
- Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.

## What to expect?
- Services are provided at either a stay-in facility or a day centre.
- Subsidies are available depending on the [means-test](https://aic.sg/care-services/home-medical#faq) result.
  - Only applicable for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents
