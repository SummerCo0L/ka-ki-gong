# SOS Suicide Crisis Intervention Programme

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SVC-SSCIP*

Provides confidential emotional support to individuals facing a crisis, thinking about suicide or affected by suicide through helpline, face to face in-person, and other support services.

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Free, Suicide Intervention

## About this support
SOS Suicide Crisis Intervention Programme comprises:

- 24-hour Hotline
- CareText
- Intervention outcalls
- Specialist counselling for individuals in crisis and persons grieving a suicide loss
- Support groups for individuals with prior suicide attempts and active suicidality
- The Programme serves self-referrals, as well as referrals from partner agencies (i.e. police, hospitals, social service agencies) and concerned third parties.

## Who is this for?
Persons at risk of suicide

## How to apply?
Reach out to SOS via the 24/7 Hotline: 1767; or CareText: WhatsApp 9151 1767.

## What to expect?
Clients can access SOS Hotline calling 1767, a 24-hour Hotline run by trained volunteers and staff. Depending on suicide risk, clients may be encouraged to seek immediate support from the hospitals (e.g. IMH), and/or receive community services or further counselling support at SOS. For callers with immediate suicide risk, SOS will activate their mobile crisis response team ("Active Rescue") to coordinate and deliver emergency intervention, led by a Crisis Support Supervisor on duty.
