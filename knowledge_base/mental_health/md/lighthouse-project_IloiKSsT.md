# Lighthouse Project

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/IloiKSsT*

Provides psychotherapy, psychological assessment and case management for youths aged 12–25 with complex moderate to high mental health needs.

**Who it targets:** Vulnerable
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Free, Referral required

## About this support
The Lighthouse Project aims to be a beacon of light that attracts and correctly directs youths (aged 12-25 years old) with complex moderate to high mental health needs to accessible and appropriate help by doing the following:

- Provides psychotherapy (e.g. cognitive behavioural therapy) for youths with complex moderate to high mental health needs.
- Conducts psychological assessments (e.g. UCLA PTSD scale) to evaluate and monitor clients.
- Provides case management including referral to hospitals, case conferencing, and handholding into hospital-based treatment.
- Right-sites youths requiring higher-tiered care into primary, secondary, or tertiary care using the therapeutic relationship built with Limitless practitioners.
- Improves community standards of care through clinical supervision and training of partner organisations by Limitless' psychologists.

## Who is this for?
Youths aged 12-25 with moderate to high mental health needs.

## How to apply?
Clients need to be referred by service providers or restructured hospitals. Self-referrals are also accepted and assessed for eligibility.

Contact Limitless at *SCAPE, 2 Orchard Link, #04-06 grovve, Singapore 237978. Operating hours: Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm.

## What to expect?
Clients would need to be referred to the programme. Lighthouse project staff would then make contact with the youth for assessment and basic emotional support. Youths may then be referred further. The duration and frequency of sessions would depend on clinician assessment.
