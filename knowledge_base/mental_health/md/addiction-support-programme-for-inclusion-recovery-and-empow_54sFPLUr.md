# Addiction Support Programme for Inclusion, Recovery, and Empowerment (ASPIRE)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/54sFPLUr*

For individuals aged 10 and above who have a present or past addiction issue (including gaming, compulsive gambling, shoplifting, drugs, alcohol, sex and porn use) or are currently impacted by the addiction of a loved one.

**Who it targets:** Families, Vulnerable
**Citizenship:** SG, OTHERS, PR
**Keywords:** Addiction, Counselling

## About this support
Addiction Support Programme for Inclusion, Recovery and Empowerment (ASPIRE) provides screening, intake, assessment, and intervention, for persons and families impacted by addictions, particularly behavioural addictions. It provides professional community therapeutic services that contributes to the community integration of persons and families affected by addictions.

## Who is this for?
Singapore citizens and persons residing in Singapore who are:

1. assessed to have a present or past addiction issue (including gaming, compulsive gambling, shoplifting, drugs, alcohol, sex and porn use), with the expressed desire to seek help OR
2. currently impacted by the addiction of a loved one.

## How to apply?
You may call, walk-in, or email our various agencies for requests on counselling. You may also be referred from other organisations and services. Information about the Addiction Support Service is available via the agencies’ website and brochures.

## What to expect?
Individuals who seek help can expect in-person and virtual counselling. These can take place via individual sessions, family sessions or group sessions. If referred, individuals are screened for service eligibility within 3 weeks after case is allocated/assigned.
