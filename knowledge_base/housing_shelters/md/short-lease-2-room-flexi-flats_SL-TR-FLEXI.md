# Short-lease 2-room Flexi Flats

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/SL-TR-FLEXI*

An affordable housing option for senior citizens (and their spouses) aged 55 and above who wish to right-size and continue living independently.

## Highlights
- Monetisation option to supplement retirement needs
- Flexibility to choose a lease ranging from 15 to 45 years (in 5-year increments) as long as it lasts the applicant(s) and their spouse(s) (if any) until at least age 95

**Who it targets:** Seniors
**Keywords:** HDB households, Home ownership, Seniors

## About this support
- Comes with pre-installed senior-friendly fittings such as grab bars and easy-to-slide partitions to separate living and bedroom spaces for privacy
- Applicant(s) may apply for 1 priority scheme to enhance their chances in the computer ballot for a short-lease 2-room Flexi flat:
  - [Senior Priority Scheme (SPS) (near existing home)](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/bto-sbf-and-open-booking-of-flats/process-for-buying-a-new-flat/application/priority-schemes?anchor=sps)
  - [Family Care Scheme (FCS) (Proximity)](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/bto-sbf-and-open-booking-of-flats/process-for-buying-a-new-flat/application/priority-schemes?anchor=fcs-proximity)
  - [Family Care Scheme (FCS) (Joint Balloting)](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/bto-sbf-and-open-booking-of-flats/process-for-buying-a-new-flat/application/priority-schemes?anchor=fcs-joint-balloting)
- Eligible first-timers may apply for the Enhanced CPF Housing Grant ([Families](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/flat-grant-and-loan-eligibility/couples-and-families/enhanced-cpf-housing-grant) or [Singles](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/flat-grant-and-loan-eligibility/singles/enhanced-cpf-housing-grant))
- Eligible buyers may apply for the [Silver Housing Bonus (SHB)](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/retirement-planning/monetising-flat-for-retirement/silver-housing-bonus) if they are selling their current flat or private housing with an Annual Value not exceeding $21,000 and buying a 3-room or smaller flat

## Who is this for?
- Applicant must be a Singapore Citizen (SC)
- If you are applying with your spouse, or parent(s)/child(ren), at least one of them must be a SC or Singapore Permanent Resident (SPR)
- If your spouse is not an SC or SPR, they must be included as a core occupier
- All applicant(s) and their spouse(s) (if any) must be at least 55 years old at the time of HFE letter application
- Monthly household income should not exceed $16,000

**Other conditions**

- If any person listed in the application owns or has an interest in any HDB flat or private residential property, the property must be disposed of within 6 months from the completion of the flat purchase.
- Applicant(s) and their spouse(s) (if any) are not eligible to buy a short-lease 2-room Flexi flat if they had previously enjoyed 2 or more housing subsidies, one of which was a Studio Apartment, or a short-lease 2-room Flexi flat or Community Care Apartment (CCA).

**Additional amount payable**

Some applicants will have to make an additional payment on top of the flat price e.g. resale levy. Visit [HDB InfoWeb](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/flat-grant-and-loan-eligibility/seniors) for more details.

## How to apply?
- Apply for an [HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/web/bp27/PrelimHFE/) early to be informed of your eligibility to buy a short-lease 2-room Flexi flat and the amounts of CPF housing grants you are eligible for.
- Visit the [HDB Flat Portal](http://homes.hdb.gov.sg/) for details on the flats to be offered on our upcoming Build-To-Order (BTO) exercise. For Sale of Balance Flats (SBF) exercise, details on the flats for sale will only be made available during the sales exercise.
- With a valid HFE letter indicating your eligibility for a short-lease 2-room Flexi flat, apply online via the [HDB Flat Portal](https://homes.hdb.gov.sg/home/landing) during the application period of a sales exercise.
- From 4 weeks after the release of the ballot results, you will be invited to book a flat.
- Within 9 months after booking a flat, you will be invited to sign the Agreement for Lease.
- When your booked flat is ready, you will be invited to collect the keys.
