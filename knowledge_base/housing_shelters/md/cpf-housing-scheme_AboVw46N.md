# CPF Housing Scheme 

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/AboVw46N*

Enables CPF members to use their Ordinary Account (OA) savings to buy properties and pay for various housing purchase-related costs if they are owners of the property.

## Highlights
- Use CPF Ordinary Account (OA) savings to buy properties or pay for housing purchase-related costs 

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Keywords:** CPF, Home ownership

## About this support
- Enables members to use their OA savings to buy residential property in Singapore
- OA savings can be used to cover downpayments, housing loans, stamp and legal fees and Home Protection Scheme premiums (for HDB flats)

## Who is this for?
- The amount of OA savings members can use depends on several factors, including the remaining lease of the property, the type of housing loan you’re taking, the age of the youngest owner using CPF, and the number of properties you are using CPF for
- For detailed information on the terms and conditions, please refer to the [terms and conditions for use of CPF savings under the CPF Housing Scheme](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/tnc/t-c-for-use-of-cpf-under-cpf-housing-scheme)
- Upon sale, transfer or disposal of the property (during your lifetime), you are required to refund the CPF principal amount withdrawn together with the accrued interest to your CPF account(s)
- If you are aged 55 or above and have pledged the property to make up your retirement sum, you will also be required to refund the pledged amount

## How to apply?
- To use your OA savings for the property, HDB or the lawyer acting for you in the purchase must submit an application to the CPF Board with your CPF withdrawal details, along with any required documentation
- [Know how much OA you can use](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/home-ownership/using-your-cpf-to-buy-a-home)
- Before your OA savings can be used, you may need to pay certain amounts in cash, [depending on the type of property and housing loan](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/can-i-use-my-cpf-savings-for-the-down-payment-of-my-property)
- Note that the savings withdrawn are paid to the property developer, HDB, sellers, banks, lawyers or such other entities on your behalf
- To adjust or stop your OA savings for your loan payments, log in to your HDB e-Services if you have an HDB loan or [myCPF](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/houseinstalment) if you have a bank loan
