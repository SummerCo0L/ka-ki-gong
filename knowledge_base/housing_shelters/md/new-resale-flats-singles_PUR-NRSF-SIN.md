# New/Resale Flats (Singles) 

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/PUR-NRSF-SIN*

Helps eligible singles decide if new or resale flats would be suitable for their consideration and provides them a holistic understanding of their housing and financing options for these flats.

## Highlights
- Eligible singles may apply for a 2-room Flexi flat from HDB or a resale flat on the open market
- Various housing grants are available in addition to the housing loan. Eligible first-timer singles may receive housing grants of up to $60,000 for new flats, and up to $115,000 for resale flats.

**Who it targets:** Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Keywords:** HDB households, Housing grants, Singles

## About this support
Eligible singles may:

- Apply for new 2-room Flexi flats (99-year lease) in all locations, across Standard, Plus and Prime projects
- Buy an Unclassified/ Standard or Plus resale flat of any size, excluding 3Gen flats
- Buy a 2-room resale Prime flat
- Access to various housing grants for eligible singles

## Who is this for?
**Singles/ Two or more singles**

All applicant(s) must:

- Be Singapore Citizen (SC) or include at least 1 other co-applicant who is an SC (if applying jointly).

**If buying a new flat**

- At least 35 years old
- Monthly household income ceiling not exceeding $8,000
- Have not taken any housing subsidy
- Must not own, or have an interest in or dispose of any private residential property (local or overseas) in the last 30 months
- An additional amount is payable if you are buying a new flat alone
- Flat types available: 2-room Flexi flat (99-year lease)

**If buying a resale flat**

- At least 21 years old (widowed or an orphan) or at least 35 years old (unmarried or divorced)
- For Unclassified/ Standard flat: No income ceiling (Income ceiling applies to qualify for HDB housing loan and CPF housing grants [excluding Proximity Housing Grant])
- For Plus flat: Monthly household income ceiling not exceeding $16,000
- For Prime flat: Monthly household income ceiling not exceeding $8,000
- Must not own, or have an interest in or dispose of any private residential property (local or overseas) in the last 30 months if you are buying a resale flat with CPF housing grants
- Flat types available:
  - All flat types (excluding 3Gen flats) in any location
  - Up to 5-room if applying for CPF housing grants

## How to apply?
- Apply for a HFE letter by visiting the [HDB Flat Portal](https://homes.hdb.gov.sg/home/landing)
- The letter will support you to acquire a holistic understanding of your housing and financing options, inform you on your eligibility to purchase new or resale flats and determine the amount of CPF housing grants and HDB housing loan you are eligible for
