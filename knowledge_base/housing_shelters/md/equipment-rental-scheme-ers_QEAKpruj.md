# Equipment Rental Scheme (ERS)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/QEAKpruj*

Subsidises the rental of essential care equipment for patients with serious illnesses.

## Highlights
- Subsidised weekly rental fees for essential medical equipment
- Covers up to two-way transport and essential consumables

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities, Seniors
**Keywords:** Home healthcare, Palliative care

## About this support
The Equipment Rental Scheme (ERS) provides patients with serious illnesses who have a prognosis of less than a year with the following subsidies:

- Weekly rental fees for approved equipment such as wheelchair/pushchair, hospital bed, oxygen concentrator, pressure relief mattress and commode
- Cost of two-way transport for delivery and collection of rented equipment

## Who is this for?
- Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- Currently receiving care from a palliative care provider, whether home-based or in a public healthcare institution (PHI).
- Diagnosed to have a serious illness with a prognosis of less than a year.
- Assessed by a qualified assessor on the type(s) of equipment required.
- *Must not be receiving subsidies for the same or similar equipment through other Government assistance schemes.

*If you have previously received Government assistance or subsidies for same or similar type of equipment prior to your ERS application, eligibility to rent equipment(s) under ERS will be assessed on a case-by-case basis.

## How to apply?
Approach the [home palliative care provider](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Home-Palliative-Care) or PHI you are receiving care from to apply for ERS.

If you are currently not under the care of any home palliative care provider or PHI, you can reach out to [Singapore Hospice Council](https://www.singaporehospice.org.sg/#contact-pop).
