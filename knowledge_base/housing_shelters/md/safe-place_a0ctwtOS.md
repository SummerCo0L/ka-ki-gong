# Safe Place

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/a0ctwtOS*

Programme aims to empower women and their families with unsupported pregnancies to make informed and life-giving choices.

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Free

## About this support
The programme provides the following services:

- Case management and counselling
- Pre- and postnatal skills equipping
- Baby essentials
- Temporary accommodation
- Referrals to other support partners

## Who is this for?
Safe Place serves pregnant women up to 32 weeks gestation who consent to receiving support services. For clients under 17 years, parental consent is required, although exceptions are considered based on individual circumstances.

## How to apply?
If you are a pregnant woman who needs help, know of one who needs help or need information about unsupported pregnancy, please contact Safe Place at 6817 4202 or text via WhatsApp at 8891 4214 to speak to the caseworkers.

For community partners, please submit a referral form and attach a social report (if available) via email ([safeplace@lakeside.org.sg](mailto:safeplace@lakeside.org.sg)).

A social worker will be in touch within 3 working days.

## What to expect?
Service users who require help with their pregnancy may contact Safe Place via email/call/WhatsApp.

Case plans and duration of case are customised to meet the unique needs and circumstances of the service users.
