# Community Care Apartments (CCAs)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/CCA*

Senior-friendly housing integrated with care and social services that can be scaled according to care needs and social activities to support seniors who are 55 and above to age independently within the community.

## Highlights
- Homes with pre-installed fittings and senior-friendly design features
- Mandatory Basic Service Package (BSP) that provides support from CCA staff, including 24-hour emergency response
- Optional paid care and support services that residents may take up based on their needs 

**Who it targets:** Seniors
**Keywords:** Home ownership

## About this support
- Each CCA unit comes with senior-friendly design features such as easy-to-slide partitions for privacy, built-in wardrobe and cabinets for storage, and a wheelchair-accessible bathroom with slip-resistant flooring and grab bars.
- Flexibility to choose a lease ranging from 15 to 45 years (in 5 year increments) as long as it lasts the applicant(s) and their spouse(s) (if any) until at least age 95.
- Eligible first-timers may apply for the Enhanced CPF Housing Grant ([Families](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/flat-grant-and-loan-eligibility/couples-and-families/enhanced-cpf-housing-grant) or [Singles](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/flat-grant-and-loan-eligibility/singles/enhanced-cpf-housing-grant)) for the flat purchase.
- All residents must subscribe to the Basic Service Package (BSP), which includes the help from a CCA staff with simple home fixes, arranging add-on care and support services, and 24-hour emergency response.
- Residents can add on optional services for assistance with Activities of Daily Living (ADL), as well as fall monitoring, housekeeping and meal services.
- Eligible CCA applicants who require permanent assistance with at least one ADL may qualify for means-tested subsidies for relevant BSP components. More details on BSP fees and subsidies are available at [Vanguard Healthcare’s website](https://www.vanguardhealthcare.com.sg/our-services/assisted-living/basic-service-package).

## Who is this for?
- Applicant must be a Singapore Citizen (SC)
- If you are applying with your spouse, or parent(s)/child(ren), at least one of them must be a SC or Singapore Permanent Resident (SPR)
- If your spouse is not an SC or SPR, they must be included as a core occupier
- All applicant(s) and their spouse(s) (if any) must be at least 55 years old at the time of HFE letter application
- Monthly household income should not exceed $16,000

**Other conditions**

- If any person listed in the application owns or has an interest in any HDB flat or private residential property, the property must be disposed of within 6 months from the completion of the flat purchase.
- Applicant(s) and their spouse(s) (if any) are not eligible to buy a CCA if they had previously enjoyed 2 or more housing subsidies, one of which was a Studio Apartment, or a short-lease 2-room Flexi flat or CCA.

**Additional amount payable**

Some applicants will have to make an additional payment on top of the flat price e.g. resale levy. Visit [HDB InfoWeb](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/bto-sbf-and-open-booking-of-flats/finding-a-new-flat/types-of-flats/community-care-apartments) for more details.

## How to apply?
- Apply for an [HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/web/bp27/PrelimHFE/) early to be informed of your eligibility to buy a CCA and the amounts of CPF housing grants you are eligible for.
- Visit the [**HDB Flat Portal**](http://homes.hdb.gov.sg/) for details on the flats to be offered on our upcoming Build-To-Order (BTO) exercise. For Sale of Balance Flats (SBF) exercise, details on the flats for sale will only be made available during the sales exercise.
- With a valid HFE letter indicating your eligibility for CCA, apply online via the [HDB Flat Portal](https://homes.hdb.gov.sg/home/landing) during the application period of a sales exercise.
- From 4 weeks after the release of the ballot results, you will be invited to book a flat.
- Within 9 months after booking a flat, you will be invited to sign the Agreement for Lease.
- When your booked flat is ready, you will be invited to sign the BSP Agreement and collect the keys.
