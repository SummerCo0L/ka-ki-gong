# MediShield Life

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/MEDISHIELD*

A basic health insurance plan managed by the CPF Board to help pay for large hospital bills and selected outpatient treatments.

## Highlights
- Lifetime coverage for all Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents regardless of age or health condition
- No application needed

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Keywords:** CPF, Health Insurance, Medical bills

## About this support
- Provides [lifetime coverage](https://moh.gov.sg/home/our-healthcare-system/medishield-life/what-is-medishield-life/what-medishield-life-benefits) for hospital bills and selected outpatient treatments
- MediShield Life coverage is sized for subsidised treatment in public hospitals and pegged at B2/C-type wards
- If you choose to stay in an A/B1-type ward or in a private hospital, you are still covered by MediShield Life
  - However, your MediShield Life payout will cover only a proportion of your bill, and you would need to draw from MediSave and/or cash to pay the balance

## Who is this for?
- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents

## How to apply?
No application is required
