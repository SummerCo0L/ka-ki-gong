# DigitalAccess@Home

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/DIGITALACCESS-HOME-SCHEME*

Provides subsidised broadband and laptops/tablets to lower-income households to support their lifestyle needs, including work and social activities.

## Highlights
- Subsidised fibre broadband for 3 years and/or subsidised laptop or tablet
- Apply online via www.digitalaccess.gov.sg or at any SG Digital community hub 

**Who it targets:** Low-income
**Keywords:** Lower-income, Subsidies

## About this support
- Subsidised fibre broadband, with monthly payment over 36 months
- Subsidised laptop or tablet, with one-time payment

## Who is this for?
To qualify for DigitalAccess@Home, you must meet all of the following criteria:

- Staying in a HDB flat
- At least one member of your household is a Singapore Citizen
- With a monthly household income (MHI) or per capita income (PCI) meeting either of the following conditions:
  Monthly household income (MHI)
  Monthly income per capita (PCI)
  No primary school student or person with disabilities in your household
  ≤ $1,900
  ≤ $650
  If there is a primary school student or person with disabilities in your household
  ≤ $3,400
  ≤ $900

You are encouraged to check your eligibility with the [online self-assessment tool](https://go.gov.sg/digitalaccesschecker) before applying.

## How to apply?
Only one application form per household is needed.

**Online submission**

- Step 1: [Visit IMDA's Digital Access Programme site](https://eservice.imda.gov.sg/das/homepage). Click on ‘For Applicant’ at the top menu bar
- Step 2: Log in with Singpass. The form will be prefilled with your information from our records. Complete the form and submit.
- Step 3: Household members have to give their consent for data collection via Singpass upon receipt of letter/SMS/email

**Hardcopy submission**

- Step 1: [Download the hardcopy](https://go.gov.sg/digitalaccess-application-form) or obtain a copy from your nearest [SG Digital community hub](https://go.gov.sg/supportgowhere-sdohubs)
- Step 2: Complete the form. Household members have to give their consent for data collection
- Step 3: Post completed form to: Info-communications Media Development Authority, 10 Pasir Panjang Road #03-01, Mapletree Business City, Singapore 117438

You will receive an update on your application via SMS/email in 10 working days. Alternatively, you can check your application status by logging into [www.digitalaccess.gov.sg](http://www.digitalaccess.gov.sg) with your SingPass.
