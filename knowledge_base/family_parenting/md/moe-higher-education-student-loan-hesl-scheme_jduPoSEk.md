# MOE Higher Education Student Loan (HESL) Scheme

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/jduPoSEk*

Loan scheme to help students who are taking MOE-subsidised diploma, undergraduate or postgraduate programmes in publicly-funded post-secondary education institutions to finance their tuition fees and borrow a living allowance loan for other education-related expenses. It also helps undergraduates to finance cost of institution-approved overseas student programme.

## Highlights
- Base provision and means-tested balance fee coverage components under the HESL scheme can cover up to 100% of subsidised tuition fees for eligible Singapore Citizen (SC) students
- Eligible students who meet the income eligibility criteria can also apply for a living allowance loan component under the HESL scheme to cover their education expenses 
- Loan is interest-free during the course of study; interest and repayment commence upon a student’s graduation or leaving the institution

**Who it targets:** Families
**Keywords:** Education expenses, Students

## About this support
For Diploma students
There are two loan components under HESL:
(i)     Base provision to cover up to 90% of subsidised tuition fees payable, depending on the student’s citizenship;
(ii)   Means-tested provisions
·  Loan to cover remaining 10% of subsidised tuition fees (for SC only)
·  Living allowance loan of up to $3,200 per academic year
For Undergraduate and Postgraduate students
There are two loan components under HESL:
(i)     Base provision to cover up to 90% of subsidised tuition fees payable, depending on the student’s citizenship;
(ii)   Means-tested provisions
· Loan to cover remaining 10% of subsidised tuition fees (for SC undergraduates only)
· Living allowance loan of up to $4,100 per academic year (for full-time undergraduate students only)
· Loan to cover up to $12,000 for the cost of one institution-approved overseas student programme (for full-time SC undergraduates only)

## Who is this for?
The HESL will be available to the following students, and the loan components available will depend on the student’s citizenship and level of study:

- SC, Singapore Permanent Resident (PR) and international student (IS) studying full-time MOE-subsidised diploma courses in any of the local polytechnics.
- SC and PR studying full-time MOE-subsidised diploma courses in Institute of Technical Education (ITE).
- SC and PR studying full-time MOE-subsidised diploma and undergraduate programmes in LASALLE College of the Arts (LASALLE), Nanyang Academy of Fine Arts (NAFA) and University of the Arts Singapore (UAS).
- SC, PR and IS studying full-time MOE-subsidised undergraduate, full-time MOE-subsidised postgraduate by coursework, and full-time/part-time MOE-subsidised postgraduate by research programmes in autonomous universities.
- SC studying part-time MOE-subsidised undergraduate programmes in autonomous universities.

Depending on the level of study and type of institution, the means-tested loan provisions are for:

- SC and PR with gross monthly household per capita income (PCI)^ of $3,500 or less.
- IS with gross monthly household PCI of $1,875 or less.

^Monthly PCI is computed based on total monthly gross household income (GHI) divided by number of family members living in the same household.

## How to apply?
- The following students can apply for HESL via the new [StudentLoanSG (SLSG)](https://go.gov.sg/slsg) portal from July 2026.
  - Newly matriculated students in Academic Year (AY) 2026 intake; and
  - Existing students (before AY2026 intake) who do not have Tuition Fee Loan (TFL) and Study Loan (SL) for their current course of study.
- Refer to the respective institution website for details on application window period and application procedures.
- Existing students who have already taken up TFL and/or SL will continue to receive their current loan provision.  No action is required from them.  However, if they wish to apply for new or additional loan provision under the HESL scheme, they should first approach their institution for advice and assessment of needs.
