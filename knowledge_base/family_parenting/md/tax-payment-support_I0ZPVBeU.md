# Tax Payment Support

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/I0ZPVBeU*

Supports eligible individuals in their tax payments through various payment arrangements such as longer GIRO-based instalment plans.

## Highlights
- Enables longer payment plans for applicants experiencing difficulties in paying their taxes 
- Grants retirees the Extended Giro Scheme for Residential Property (Retirees), offering longer instalment plans for tax payment

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Keywords:** Long-term, Tax relief

## About this support
- Longer instalment plans may be made available for individuals or businesses facing financial challenges or other extenuating circumstances
- Eligible retirees may apply for the Extended Giro Scheme for Residential Property (Retirees), which provides a longer instalment plan of up to 24 months for property tax payment

## Who is this for?
For individuals who experience unexpected or extenuating circumstances due to the following:

- Financial issues
- High medical expenses
- Loss of employment or income
- Lawsuits
  - You may be required to submit documentary evidence to support your request for a longer instalment plan

For retirees:

- To be eligible for the Extended Giro Scheme for Residential Property (Retirees), applicants must meet all the following conditions:
  - You are residing in the property (and taxed at owner-occupier tax rates)
  - Your assessable income for Year of Assessment 2023 does not exceed $34,000
  - All property owners, including yourself, are aged 65 or above in 2024
  - There is outstanding property tax payable for the property

If applicants cease to be eligible, their extended instalment plan will be reverted to the standard instalment plan

## How to apply?
For individuals:
- Apply for an extended instalment plan via the 'Apply for Payment Plan' digital service through the IRAS [myTax Portal](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg)
  - Please note that instalment plans are only available with GIRO
- For existing GIRO users, cancel your current plan and set up a new one. You may refer to this guide on [cancelling and reactivating GIRO for detailed instructions](https://www.iras.gov.sg/media/docs/default-source/uploadedfiles/pdf/steps-to-cancel-and-reactivate-giro---enf-webpages@18feb2020.pdf?sfvrsn=fbc17716_5)
- For applicants without a GIRO arrangement for Individual Income and Property Tax, you may apply for GIRO (with a DBS / POSB or OCBC bank account) via [myTax Portal](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg) and set up your new plan online. Refer to [this guide](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/property-tax/property-owners/payments/experiencing-difficulties-in-paying-your-tax) for detailed instructions
For businesses without an existing GIRO arrangement for Corporate Income Tax, Property Tax and Goods and Services Tax:
- Apply for GIRO with a DBS / POSB, OCBC, UOB, Bank of China or Maybank account via the IRAS [myTax Portal](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg)
- Arrange a new plan
- Alternatively, [you may refer to this guide for the detailed steps](https://www.iras.gov.sg/media/docs/default-source/uploadedfiles/pdf/eservice-user-guide_apply-for-payment-plan_non-individuals_2023-11.pdf?sfvrsn=31c2fac6_3)

Where you do not have account with any of the participating banks listed above, you may:

- Fill up [this form ](https://form.gov.sg/673c038018eb14d441d54153)to request for GIRO application form
- View on applying GIRO [here](https://www.iras.gov.sg/quick-links/payments/giro-individual-income-tax)

Please refer to [IRAS’ site on alternative tax payment plans for more details](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/property-tax/property-owners/payments/experiencing-difficulties-in-paying-your-tax)
