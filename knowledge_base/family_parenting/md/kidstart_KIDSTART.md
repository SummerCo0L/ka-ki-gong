# KidSTART

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/KIDSTART*

Supports pregnant mothers and lower-income families in early childhood development, to build strong foundations for children aged 6 and below.

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income
**Citizenship:** SG
**Keywords:** Free, Lower-income, Resources and Support

## About this support
KidSTART Singapore Limited is a non-profit organisation and an Institution of a Public Character that specialises in supporting families in early childhood development.

Our specialist team partners with the government and community to empower lower-income families in building strong foundations for their children, giving them a good start in life.

## Who is this for?
You are eligible for our services if your family meets these criteria:

1. You are a pregnant mother and/or have a child who is a Singapore Citizen and aged 6 and below
2. You have a gross monthly household income of $2,500 and below, or per capita  income  of $650 and below

[Learn more about this programme and how it can help you](https://kidstart.sg/be-a-kidstart-family/?utm_source=supportgowhere&utm_medium=link&utm_campaign=supportgowhere)

## How to apply?
[Fill up the application form](https://go.gov.sg/beakidstartfamily) to sign up as a KidSTART Family.

## What to expect?
- Children achieve age-appropriate child development outcomes
- Parent-child interactions and family functioning improve
