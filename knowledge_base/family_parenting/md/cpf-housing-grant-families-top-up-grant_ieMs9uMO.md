# CPF Housing Grant (Families) - Top-Up Grant

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/ieMs9uMO*

Provides up to $40,000 housing grants to eligible families if they had previously received CPF Housing Grant for Singles and now qualify for the CPF Housing Grant for Families.

## Highlights
- Up to $40,000 in Top-Up grant for eligible families 
- Available to those who previously received a Singles Grant or bought a 2-room Flexi flat as a single, and are now married
- Grant is directly credited into all eligible Singapore Citizen and Singapore Permanent Resident members’ CPF Ordinary Accounts

**Who it targets:** Families
**Keywords:** Housing grants

## About this support
- Up to $40,000 in Top-Up Grant
- The applicant applying for the Top-Up Grant may also apply for other grants (e.g. Enhanced Housing Grant and Proximity Housing Grant), if eligible

## Who is this for?
- You must be a Singapore Citizen (SC) aged 21 and above, and your spouse or child is a SC or Singapore Permanent Resident (SPR)
- You have previously received the CPF Housing Grant for Resale Flats (Singles) or bought a 2-room Flexi flat from HDB as a single, with other single citizens, or with a non-resident spouse. You must not have taken any [**housing subsidy**](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/bto-sbf-and-open-booking-of-flats/conditions-after-buying-a-new-flat?anchor=housing-subsidy) other than the Singles Grant or the purchase of a 2-room Flexi flat from HDB.
- Your average gross monthly household income must not exceed:
  - $16,000 if you were a recipient of a Singles Grant who bought an HDB resale flat, or you bought a short-lease 2-room Flexi flat
  - $8,000 if you bought a 2-room Flexi flat on a 99-year lease
- You and your spouse, as well as other persons listed in the application, must not have ownership or interest in any private residential property, local or overseas, and no disposal of such property within the last 30 months prior to the application
- You and your spouse, as well as other persons listed in the application, must not have ownership or interest in more than one non-residential private property, local or overseas, and no disposal of such property within the last 30 months prior to the application

## How to apply?
You must [submit your application for Top-Up Grant](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/AB22AWTopUpGrantWeb/AB22PMain) within 6 months of your marriage registration or spouse/ child becoming a SC/ SPR
