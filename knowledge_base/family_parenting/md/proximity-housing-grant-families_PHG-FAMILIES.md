# Proximity Housing Grant (Families)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/PHG-FAMILIES*

Provides up to $30,000 for families buying a resale flat to live with or near parents or children.

## Highlights
- Grant of $30,000 to live with your parents or child
- Grant of $20,000 to live near your parents or child (within 4km) 
- If applicant is living near them, parents/child may reside in either a HDB flat or private residential property to be eligible for PHG

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Keywords:** Families, Home ownership, Housing grants

## About this support
- $30,000 grant to live with your parents or child
- $20,000 grant to live near your parents or child (if resale flat is within 4km)

## Who is this for?
- Singapore Citizen (SC) or include at least 1 other SC or Singapore Permanent Resident (SPR)
- At least 21 years
- [Applicants buying a resale flat to live with, or within 4 km of, their parents or child](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/flat-grant-and-loan-eligibility/couples-and-families/proximity-housing-grant)
- Have not received the Proximity Housing Grant (PHG) before

## How to apply?
- Obtain a HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter through the [HDB Flat Portal](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/web/bp27/PrelimHFE/) to check your eligibility for grants and loans
- If you meet the eligibility criteria, you may apply for the grant when submitting your resale application
