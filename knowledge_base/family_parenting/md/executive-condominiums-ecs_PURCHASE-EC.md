# Executive Condominiums (ECs) 

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/PURCHASE-EC*

Provides a more affordable option to eligible Singaporean households who aspire to own private housing.

## Highlights
- ECs are strata-titled and are developed and sold by private developers
- With design features and facilities similar to private condominiums

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Keywords:** HDB households, Home ownership

## About this support
- Eligible households can purchase an EC unit from a property developer at a subsidised rate
- Eligible first-timer households may qualify for the CPF Housing Grant

## Who is this for?
**For fiancé or fiancée, married couples and/ or parent(s) with child(ren), orphaned siblings:**

- Applicant must:
  - Be a Singapore Citizen (SC)
  - Include at least 1 other SC or Singapore Permanent Resident (SPR)
  - Be at least 21 years old

**For 2 or more singles applying jointly:**

- All applicants must be:
  - SCs
  - At least 35 years old

**Monthly household income ceiling:**

- The total income of all persons listed in the EC application for new units in EC projects with tenders closing on or after 24 August 2026 must not exceed $18,000*
  *The income ceiling for new units in EC sites with tenders awarded before 24 August 2026 is $16,000.

**Private residential property:**

- Must not own, have an interest in or dispose of any private residential property (local or overseas) in the last 30 months

**Housing subsidies and Resale Levy:**

- If any core applicants/ occupiers have taken a housing subsidy, they are considered second-timers.
- All second-timer core applicants / occupiers will need to pay their respective [resale levies](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/bto-sbf-and-open-booking-of-flats/conditions-after-buying-a-new-flat?anchor=resale-levy) if applicable, when buying an EC unit from a property developer
- If any core applicants/ occupiers have taken 2 housing subsidies, they are not eligible to apply for or be listed as a core occupier in an EC application

## How to apply?
- Submit your [application](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/executive-condominiums/process-for-buying-an-ec) when the EC project is launched
- Contact the property developer directly for details on the application process and required documents
- The property developer will inform you of the outcome of the application
