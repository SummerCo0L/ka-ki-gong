# Priority Scheme - First-Timer (Parents & Married Couples), FT(PMC) Category 

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/FTPMC-CAT*

To help young families settle down more quickly, additional support will be provided for applicants who qualify for the FT(PMC) category.

## Highlights
- Additional ballot chance for the Build-To-Order (BTO) or Sale of Balance (SBF) exercises, totalling 3 ballot chances for FT(PMC) applicants
- First priority for applicants applying for 4-room or smaller BTO flats in Standard projects under the Family and Parenthood Priority Scheme (FPPS)

**Who it targets:** Families
**Keywords:** HDB households, Homes

## About this support
- 1 additional ballot chance, on top of the 2 provided to First-Timer Families, when applying for BTO or SBF flats
- Receive first priority for flats set aside under the Family and Parenthood Priority Scheme (FPPS) when applying for a 4-room or smaller BTO flat in Standard projects
- Learn more about the additional ballot chances that you may receive as a first-timer family when applying for a flat during a [BTO or SBF exercise](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/bto-sbf-and-open-booking-of-flats/process-for-buying-a-new-flat/modes-of-sales).

## Who is this for?
Applicants must meet all of the following criteria:

- Families with at least 1 Singapore Citizen child aged 18 and below, or married couples aged 40 and below (includes married couples who are expecting and have a doctor’s certification of the pregnancy)
- Never owned or sold a residential property before, including a HDB flat
- Did not have a chance to book a BTO or SBF flat in the past 5 years prior to their flat application

## How to apply?
- Obtain a HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter through the [HDB Flat Portal](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/web/bp27/PrelimHFE/) and apply for a flat during BTO or SBF exercises
- You need not request to be considered for FT (PMC). HDB will assess and inform you about your eligibility after you have submitted your flat application
