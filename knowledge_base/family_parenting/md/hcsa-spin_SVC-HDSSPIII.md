# HCSA SPIN

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SVC-HDSSPIII*

SPIN supports single parents and lone caregivers through casework and counselling, social support network such as befriending, practical support and an online portal of information and resources.

**Who it targets:** Families
**Citizenship:** SG, PR
**Keywords:** Free, Resources and Support

## About this support
SPIN aims to empower single parents and lone caregivers to be resilient and self-reliant, and to help them attain increased capacity in caregiving/parenting, improve their social support, psychological well-being and increase their access to practical resources.

Services include:

- Needs assessment by social workers and co-creation of individualised care plans with single parents/lone caregivers to address identified needs.
- Counselling for single parents and children who have been assessed to be in need for emotional and mental health support.
- Befriending and peer support for single parents to build social support and trust amongst other parents and volunteers
- Counselling, tuition and homework support for the children (aged 7 to 17) of the clients
- Workshops and events to empower single parents with essential life and career skills
- Practical support such as referrals to resource channels to support clients in meeting identified needs, disbursement of grocery vouchers and food items

## Who is this for?
- Single parents or lone caregivers of children below 18 years old, and these children
- No income criteria and no geographical boundaries

## How to apply?
You may

1. Approach any Family Service Centre (FSC) for referral, or
2. Sign up online at https://crm.hcsa.org.sg/spin-member-application-form/ to fill up the online registration form, or
3. Call or email the programme for assistance in signing up.

## What to expect?
A social work associate will respond to the enquiry/referral within 3-5 working days and arrange for intake assessment. Thereafter, a social worker will be assigned to the parent, and trained befrienders will be matched to the parent. The programme is for a maximum period of 12 months. After the programme ends, the parent will be invited to join the Alumni Chapter to continue staying connected with a community and contribute to a supportive network of single-parent families.
