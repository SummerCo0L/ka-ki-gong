# Healthier SG

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/HEALTHIERSG*

A national strategy focused on preventive care to empower individuals to take steps toward better health, with the support of their Healthier SG clinic and community partners.

## Highlights
- Receive dedicated care from your preferred Healthier SG clinic
- Prevent or manage chronic conditions and complications
- Create your Health Plan with your family doctor
- Receive healthcare and medication subsidies and benefits

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Keywords:** Health screenings, Subsidies, Vaccination

## About this support
Benefits after enrolment include:

- Fully subsidised first Health Plan consultation and subsequent annual Health Plan check-ins with your enrolled Healthier SG clinic
- Healthpoints (worth $20) via Healthy 365 app after the first Health Plan consultation
- One-off Healthpoints (worth $10) via Healthy 365 app upon completing both an in-person annual Health Plan check-in and viewing of Health Plan on HealthHub within the qualifying period of 1 July 2026 to 31 December 2029.
- Special subsidies for Healthier SG screening tests and vaccinations (for eligible Singapore Citizens)
- Healthier SG Chronic Tier subsidy at Healthier SG clinics for CHAS cardholders with high medication requirements (for selected chronic medications at prices comparable to polyclinic prices)
- Full MediSave coverage for treatment of chronic conditions listed under the Chronic Disease Management Programme (CDMP), up to the relevant MediSave withdrawal limit

## Who is this for?
Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents aged 40 years old and above to enrol with a Healthier SG clinic or polyclinic

## How to apply?
**Self-enrol via HealthHub**

If you are eligible, you will be invited via SMS to enrol in Healthier SG. You can do so in three simple steps:

1. Use the HealthHub app, which can be downloaded from Google Play or Apple App Store.
2. Choose your preferred Healthier SG clinic from the [list of more than 1,000 Healthier SG clinics](https://gowhere.gov.sg/healthiersg).
3. Book an appointment for your fully subsidised first Health Plan consultation.

For a detailed step-by-step guide on how to enrol, click [here](https://www.healthiersg.gov.sg/enrolment/guide/).

If you have not received an SMS invitation, you can:

1. Request one by submitting your details at https://go.gov.sg/healthiersg-sms-invite
2. Contact the MOH hotline at 6325 9220

**Enrol with caregiver's assistance**

You can grant your caregiver/family member/loved one access to your HealthHub, so they can use their smartphone to help you enrol in Healthier SG. Follow the step-by-step guide at these links:

- [Grant your caregiver/family member access](https://safe.menlosecurity.com/https:/www.healthiersg.gov.sg/enrolment/addcaregiver/)
- [Assist your family member’s enrolment](https://safe.menlosecurity.com/https:/www.healthiersg.gov.sg/enrolment/caregivers/)

**Other assisted enrolment**

- Visit any ServiceSG Centre or AIC Link for enrolment assistance. To find an enrolment station near you, visit [HealthierSGGoWhere](https://www.gowhere.gov.sg/healthiersg/)
