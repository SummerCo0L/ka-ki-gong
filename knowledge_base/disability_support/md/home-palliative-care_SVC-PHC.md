# Home Palliative Care

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SVC-PHC*

Support caregivers in caring for patients with life-limiting illnesses at home.

**Who it targets:** Seniors
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Caregiver support, Referral required

## About this support
Provides end-of-life care services to improve the quality of life for patients with life-limiting illness at home, supporting both patients and caregivers with:

- Medical and nursing support to manage pain and symptoms
- Emotional and social support
- Caregiver training
- Equipment loan
- Advance Care Planning
- Care coordination
- 24/7 clinical services
- Bereavement care

## Who is this for?
Home-bound patient diagnosed by a doctor to have an advanced life-limiting illness with a prognosis less than 1 year.

## How to apply?
- You can apply:
  ·       Through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one’s condition and needs
  ·       At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)
  If you are interested to view the list of service providers, visit the [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Home-Palliative-Care).
  For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline[1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).
  Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.
  - Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.

## What to expect?
Charges may apply. Please check with the service provider.
