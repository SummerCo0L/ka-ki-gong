# Inpatient Hospice Palliative Care Service (IHPCS)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SVC-IHPCS*

Supports end-of-life patients who cannot be cared for at home and require inpatient care.

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities, Seniors
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Palliative care, Referral required

## About this support
Provides medical and emotional support for end-of-life patients who cannot be cared for at home and their caregivers, including services provided by specialised care teams such as:

- Specialist palliative care focusing on the management of symptoms such as pain, breathlessness, nausea and vomiting
- Emotional, spiritual and grief support and counselling
- Palliative rehabilitation, including physiotherapy and occupational therapy
- Music and art therapy

## Who is this for?
- Patient with a prognosis of less than 3 months who needs:
  - Specialised care especially in the last phase of life, when care at home, in a nursing home or outpatient setting is not possible.

- Patient with a prognosis of less than 12 months who requires:
  - Time-limited care up to 28 calendar days for severe symptoms that cannot be managed in outpatient settings

## How to apply?
You can apply:

- Through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one’s condition and needs
- At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)

If you are interested to view the list of service providers, visit the [AIC’s website](https://aic.sg/Care-Services/Inpatient-Hospice-Palliative-Care-Services) .

For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline[1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).

- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.
- Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.

## What to expect?
- Subsidies are available depending on the [means-test](https://aic.sg/care-services/home-medical#faq) result.
  - Only applicable to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents
- MediShield Life and MediSave can be used to cover costs.
- If patients need further financial assistance, their respective service providers will be able to provide more information.
