# Our Lives Our Voices (OLOV)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/f4VjU9VH*

OLOV aims to empower clients to become self-advocates and foster an inclusive society where PWIDs can independently participate in the community in a meaningful manner. Through personalised training and peer support, it builds confidence and independence.

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities
**Citizenship:** PR, SG
**Keywords:** Education

## About this support
The programme provides structured, personalised training supported by a customised web application, peer-led facilitation, and community engagement. It equips PWIDs with essential communication and interpersonal skills, empowering them to discover their strengths, express their views, take on meaningful community roles, and pursue greater autonomy, purpose, and active participation in society.

## Who is this for?
1. PWIDs aged 18 years and above with an IQ below 70
2. PWIDs who demonstrate ability to participate in group training sessions, express basic intentions and preferences.
3. PWIDs registered under MINDS programmes

## How to apply?
Interested applicants can apply through the following:

1. Email to [olov@minds.org.sg](mailto:olov@minds.org.sg)

2.  Submit the form via https://tinyurl.com/olovinterest

3. Call 65478503

## What to expect?
1. Basic screening will be conducted to ensure that PWIDs meet the criteria
2. PWIDs will also be assessed over 2 trial sessions to determine suitability and interest.
3. Enrollment is throughout the year.
