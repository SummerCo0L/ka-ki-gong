# Gift Of A Lifetime Plus (GOAL+)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/bjQYWnuX*

Gift Of A Lifetime Plus (GOAL+) aims to provide eligible caregivers of person with disability (PWDs) with an initial capital of $5,000 to set up trust accounts with Special Needs Trust Company Pte Ltd (SNTC). Under GOAL+, the Government sponsors matching donations of up to $10,000 for low- and middle-income families who contribute to the trust account within a five-year window period.

## Highlights
- GOAL+ is a sponsorship scheme that aims to help caregivers save and provide for their dependant with disability. SG Enable Case Managers will invite eligible caregivers to apply for GOAL+ during the trust set-up process.  

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities
**Keywords:** Caregiving expenses, PWDs, Special needs

## About this support
How does GOAL+ work?

1. GOAL+ runs from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031. Eligible caregivers whose PWDs do not have a Special Needs Trust (SNT) account can receive $5,000 from Community Chest to set up one.
2. Eligible caregivers of PWDs with verified disability can qualify for matching top-ups of up to $10,000 from the Government.
3. GOAL+ allows for a five-year top-up window for eligible SNT accounts, so that caregivers can plan contributions via cash top-up to the trust account in a more sustainable manner. Applicants are strongly encouraged to top up the trust account as soon as practicable. Any contribution made beyond the five years will not be entitled to matching top-ups from the Government.

## Who is this for?
To qualify for GOAL+, PWD must meet the following criteria:

- A Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- The family has a per capita household income (PCHI) of ≤ $3,600 (P64), or annual value (AV) of property ≤ $21,000 for households with a PCHI of $0.
- Has a verified disability

## How to apply?
Before applying for GOAL+, all applicants are encouraged to:

1. Check if the PWD’s disability status has already been verified by logging into [SupportGoWhere](http://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/grants/pwdr/apply) with your Singpass. If PWD is above 21 years old and does not have Singpass, you may check his/her disability status by calling the Operator (Bizlink) at 64366635 or email to [msfdisability@bizlink.org.sg](mailto:msfdisability@bizlink.org.sg).
2. If PWD does not have a verified disability status, he/she must get a [Disability Verification Form (DVF)](https://www.enablingguide.sg/disability-verification) completed by a relevant registered Healthcare Professional*.

*Please note that a submitted DVF does not mean that PWD’s disability status has been verified.

Please contact SG Enable for an appointment with a Case Manager to:

(i)     Find out how the Special Needs Trust works

(ii)    Assess eligibility for GOAL+ sponsorship
