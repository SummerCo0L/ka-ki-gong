# Pioneer Generation Disability Assistance Scheme (PioneerDAS)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/PIONEER-DAS*

Provides Pioneers with moderate or severe functional disabilities with cash assistance for everyday expenses.

## Highlights
- Cash payouts of $100 per month for expenses
- Processing time will take between 6 to 8 weeks
- Apply through AIC eServices for Financing Schemes (eFASS)

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities, Seniors
**Keywords:** Cash assistance, Pioneer generation, PWDs

## About this support
- Cash payouts of $100 per month for expenses
- Can be used for long-term care needs, such as hiring a helper, paying for home care services, or daily expenses

## Who is this for?
- Must be a Pioneer.
- Check your loved one's Pioneer status via:
  - LifeSG App: Pioneers can view their Pioneer Generation (PG) e-card after logging in.
  - Singpass Mobile App: Seniors may log on to their SingPass mobile app to view their eligibility status.
  - [View MOH website for more information](https://www.moh.gov.sg/cost-financing/healthcare-schemes-subsidies/pioneer-generation-package)

- Lives in Singapore
- Must be assessed by an approved healthcare professional to permanently require some assistance with at least 3 of the following 6 Activities of Daily Living (ADLs):
  - Feeding
  - Washing
  - Dressing
  - Transferring
  - Toileting
  - Walking or moving around

## How to apply?
You can apply:

- Online at the [AIC eServices for Financing Schemes (eFASS)](https://efinance.aic.sg/)
- Via assistance from the nursing home your loved one is staying in
- At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)

You will need to provide:

- [Functional Assessment Report](https://www.aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/Pioneer-Generation-Disability-Assistance-Scheme#q1) from an approved healthcare professional
- NRIC details
- Bank details
