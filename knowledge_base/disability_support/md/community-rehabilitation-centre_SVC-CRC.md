# Community Rehabilitation Centre

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SVC-CRC*

Centre-based therapy sessions to help seniors improve mobility, speech, and daily living skills.

**Who it targets:** Seniors
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Day Care, Referral required

## About this support
- Provides therapy at a centre to help seniors regain and maintain their ability to do daily tasks
- Therapy provided includes:
  - Physiotherapy (for those with mobility issues due to physical disability, trauma or illness such as stroke)
  - Occupational Therapy (for those who need to learn, recover or maintain daily living and work skills)
  - Speech Therapy (for those with swallowing or speech disorders)

## Who is this for?
Senior who is diagnosed with Parkinson's disease, or had a stroke, fracture or other conditions and now require some assistance with moving, speaking or swallowing.

## How to apply?
You can apply:

- Through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one’s condition and needs
- At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)

If you are interested to view the list of service providers, visit the [AIC’s website](https://aic.sg/Care-Services/Community-Rehabilitation).

For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline[1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).

- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.
- Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.

## What to expect?
Subsidies are available depending on the [means-test](https://aic.sg/care-services/home-medical#faq) result.

- Only applicable to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents
