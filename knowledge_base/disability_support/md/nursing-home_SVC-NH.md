# Nursing Home

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SVC-NH*

A long-term residential care facility for seniors who have limited or no caregiver support and require assistance with daily nursing and living needs.

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities, Seniors
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Caregiver support, Referral required

## About this support
Provides round-the-clock nursing and care services, including:

- Help with daily living activities such as going to the toilet, showering, eating, etc
- Nursing support such as management of nasogastric tube, urine catheter, wound dressing, etc
- Physical and social activities such as maintenance exercises, outings, etc

For selected nursing homes with dementia and/or psychiatric facilities, services include:

- Programmes and activities for cognitive stimulation to slow down the deterioration of the senior’s physical and mental functions

## Who is this for?
- Senior who has a physical or mental disability because of illness
- Unable to be cared for at home by family or paid domestic worker, and has tried all other care options such as using day care, home medical, home nursing services, etc

## How to apply?
You can apply:

- Through the hospital, polyclinic or medical social worker who is familiar with your loved one’s condition and needs
- At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)

If you are interested to view the list of providers, visit the [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Nursing-Home).

For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline[1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).

- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.
- Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.

## What to expect?
- Subsidies are available depending on the [means-test](https://aic.sg/Care-Services/nursing-home#faq) result.
  - Only applicable to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents
  - Additional fees such as deposits and other charges may apply.
