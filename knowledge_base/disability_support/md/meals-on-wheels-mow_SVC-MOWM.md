# Meals on Wheels (MOW)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SVC-MOWM*

Meal deliveries for homebound clients who are unable to buy and prepare their own meals and do not have a caregiver to help them to do so.

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities, Seniors
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Home Care, Referral required

## About this support
- Delivers lunch and dinner meals 7 days a week islandwide (including public holidays)
- Caters meals to dietary requirements (halal, non-halal, vegetarian, or blended/soft food)

## Who is this for?
- Seniors who are frail and homebound
- Unable to buy and prepare their own meals, and do not have a caregiver to help them

## How to apply?
- You will need to get a referral from a hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved ones’ condition and needs.
- You may also contact the service provider for a discussion. Visit [AIC’s website](https://aic.sg/Care-Services/Meals-on-Wheels) to search for service providers by** **postal code, address or your device’s current location.
- For further assistance, contact the Agency for Integrated Care (AIC) at [1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060), email [enquiries@aic.sg](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg) or walk in to a nearby [AIC Link](https://aic.sg/Contact-Us).

If you are interested to view the list of service providers, visit the [AIC’s website.](https://aic.sg/Care-Services/Meals-on-Wheels)

For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline[1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).

- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.
- Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.

## What to expect?
Subsidies are available depending on the [means-test](https://aic.sg/Care-Services/Meals-on-wheels#faq) result.

- Only applicable to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents
