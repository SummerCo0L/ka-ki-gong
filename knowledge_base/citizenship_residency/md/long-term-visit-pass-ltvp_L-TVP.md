# Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP) 

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/L-TVP*

Eligible applicants can remain in Singapore for an extended period via a Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP).

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Citizenship:** OTHERS
**Keywords:** Families, Fees payable

## About this support
- Eligible foreigners can stay in Singapore for an extended period of time
- The LTVP will be issued digitally and can be viewed on the Singpass app or downloaded from [MyICA](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/icsbip) or via [FileSG](https://www.file.gov.sg/for-individuals)
- Singapore Citizens with foreign spouses may also be eligible for LTVP Plus (LTVP+), providing a longer period of residency and additional employment and healthcare benefits

## Who is this for?
The following applicant types are eligible to apply for LTVP:

- Spouse of a Singapore citizen (must be sponsored by the Singapore citizen spouse)^
- Foreign spouse of a Singapore Permanent Resident (must be sponsored by the Permanent Resident spouse)
- Unmarried child under 21, born within a legal marriage to / legally adopted by a Singapore citizen or Permanent Resident (must be sponsored by parents)
- Parent of a Singapore citizen or Permanent Resident (sponsor must be above 21)
- Graduate from an Institute of Higher Learning seeking employment in Singapore
- Parent or grandparent of a Student’s Pass holder studying in Singapore (requires a local sponsor above 21 years old)
- Visitor seeking to give birth in Singapore (requires a local sponsor above 21 years old)

Please note that all the above criteria are not exhaustive. Eligibility is still subject to ICA’s final approval.

- Singapore Citizens with foreign spouses may also be eligible for LTVP Plus (LTVP+), providing a longer period of residency and additional employment and healthcare benefits
- For more information on the [LTVP+ and its benefits, as well as your eligibility for it, please refer here](https://www.ica.gov.sg/news-and-publications/newsroom/media-release/351)

For LTVP+

- No separate application is required. You will automatically be considered for LTVP+ (if eligible) when you apply for LTVP

^ Couples consisting of a Singapore Citizen and a Non-Resident are strongly encouraged to complete their Pre-Marriage LTVP Assessment (PMLA) before marriage. [Learn more about the PMLA here](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/pre-marriage-long-term-visit-pass-assessment) and [apply for PMLA here](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/esvclandingpage/evpaa).

## How to apply?
- Submit your application online using the [e-Service](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/esvclandingpage/evp)
- After receiving the in-principle approval notice for your LTVP application, refer to the [various procedures relating to the Completion of Formalities and the Issuance of Long-Term Visit Pass ](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/LTVP/cof-and-issue-new)for the next steps
- For further assistance, please contact ICA at [6391 6100](tel:6391-6100) or [submit an enquiry](https://go.gov.sg/ica-ltvp-enquiry)

## What to expect?
- A S$45 non-refundable processing fee is applicable for each application
- Additional fees will apply if the LTVP application is granted in-principle approval
- Applications are generally processed within six weeks, provided all required documents are submitted and in order
- It may take longer for couples who did not complete the PMLA (up to 6 months)
- You will be informed of the application outcome via email and may also check the application status online through [MyICA](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/icsbip) or the [e-Service](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/esvclandingpage/evp)
