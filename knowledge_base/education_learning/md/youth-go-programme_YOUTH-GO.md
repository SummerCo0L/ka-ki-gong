# Youth GO! Programme

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/YOUTH-GO*

An engagement and intervention programme for youths who may benefit from additional support (aged 12-21) that helps them stay focused in their studies/work, remain crime-free, develop problem-solving skills and be resilient.

**Who it targets:** Families
**Citizenship:** SG, PR
**Keywords:** Referral required, Youths at risk

## About this support
- Youth GO! is an engagement and intervention programme that supports youths who may benefit from additional support.
- Its objectives are to help youths who may benefit from additional support be meaningfully engaged in their studies or work, stay crime-free, develop problem-solving skills and be resilient individuals.

## Who is this for?
Youths who:

- are Singapore Citizens/Permanent Residents (PR)
- are between 12 - 21 years old
- Diplay the following:
  - Pro-criminal attitude/behaviour;
  - Physically/verbally aggressive behaviour; and/or
  - Subject to negative peer influence

Youths who had completed the Guidance Programme (GP), Streetwise Programme (SWP), Youth Enhanced Supervision (YES) and/probation but require further support, and youths who are out-of-school and not engaged in meaningful work, can be eligible for the YGP, subject to meeting the above criteria.

## How to apply?
- Referrals can be made by contacting the respective Youth GO! service provider according to the region of the youth's residential address or the hotspot area.
- Please refer to [Youth GO! Programme webpage](https://www.msf.gov.sg/what-we-do/help-those-in-need/article/programmes/Youth-GO-Programme) for information on the regions served by the different service providers, and their contact information.

## What to expect?
- For hotspot referrals, Youth GO! workers will work towards befriending the youth to assess if they fit the criteria of the target clientele.
- Where referrals are successful, Youth GO! workers will continue to engage the youth and conduct casework and intervention through positive engagement activities and case counselling sessions.
