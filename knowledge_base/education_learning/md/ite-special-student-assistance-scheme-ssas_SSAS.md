# ITE Special Student Assistance Scheme (SSAS)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/SSAS*

One-off financial assistance to help students tide over short-term financial difficulties.

## Highlights
- One-off financial assistance from $100 to $400
- Apply online via the ITE portal. Click on iStudent icon > Financial Assistance icon.

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income
**Keywords:** Cash assistance, Students

## About this support
- One-off financial assistance from $100 to $400
- The amount will depend on the needs of each individual student

## Who is this for?
Full-time Singaporean ITE students who require financial assistance to tide over short-term financial difficulties

## How to apply?
You can apply online via the [ITE portal](https://myite.ite.edu.sg/)

If you need more help, refer to the [step by step guide](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/145/c7a7f637-986d-483a-9766-a19bccd726c0/Step By Step Guide on Applying Financial Assistance_Bursary_15 Sep 25.pdf).
