# Silver Housing Bonus (SHB)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/SHB*

Seniors aged 55 or above may receive cash bonus when they right-size to a 3-room or smaller HDB flat, and top-up the proceeds from the sale of their existing property into their CPF Retirement Account (RA) and join CPF LIFE.

## Highlights
- Receive CPF monthly payouts for as long as they live, under CPF LIFE
- Application must be submitted within 1 year from date of completion of the second housing transaction

**Who it targets:** Seniors
**Keywords:** Cash assistance, CPF payouts

## About this support
- With effect from 1 December 2025, the SHB has been enhanced to provide greater support to seniors.
- Seniors can supplement their retirement income if they right-size from a HDB flat or private residential property with an Annual Value (AV) up to $31,000 and buy a 3-room or smaller HDB flat.
- Seniors who commit to a net increase of up to $60,000* in their CPF RA after right-sizing, with the sum going towards their retirement payouts, can qualify for an SHB amount of $30,000. They can do so using their CPF housing refunds**, if sufficient, and may no longer need to make the top-up in cash.
- They can qualify for an additional $10,000 cash bonus if they right-size to a 2-room or smaller flat, including a Community Care Apartment (CCA). This additional $10,000 bonus applies regardless of the amount they commit to their CPF RA.
- Overall, the maximum SHB amount that eligible seniors may receive is $40,000. Visit [HDB InfoWEB](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/retirement-planning/monetising-flat-for-retirement/silver-housing-bonus) for more details

* The net increase to commit in your CPF RA depends on the net sale proceeds and is capped at $60,000 per household. It is further capped by the prevailing Full Retirement Sum (FRS).

** CPF principal amount withdrawn to purchase a property and the accrued interest that needs to be refunded after selling the property.

## Who is this for?
- At least 1 owner must be a Singapore Citizen (SC)
- The SC is aged 55 or above
- Monthly household income not more than $16,000
- Existing property is an HDB flat (met minimum occupation period for resale flat), or a private residential property with an Annual Value of up to $31,000
- No concurrent ownership or interest in a private residential property or in more than 1 non-residential property

Other conditions:

- Next property you are buying is a 3-room (excluding 3-room terrace) or smaller flat from HDB or from the resale market
- You must have positive net sale proceeds
- Booking of new HDB flat or application to buy resale flat must either be before sale of existing residential property, or within 12 months of completing the sale of the existing residential property
- SHB application must be submitted within 1 year from the legal completion date of the second housing transaction (either the purchase or sale)

## How to apply?
- Learn about your eligibility for this scheme (and other housing monetisation options) and the estimated monthly payouts you can receive via [HDB’s 'Check Housing Monetisation Option' e-Service](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/BR05AWEnquireHMOptions)
- To apply:
  - Enquire with our Customer Relations Executive during the appointment for second housing transaction (either resale completion or new flat keys collection appointment)
  - HDB will then schedule a financial counselling session where all the details and information such as the CPF top-up amount, bonus and the monthly payout amount would be shared during the session
