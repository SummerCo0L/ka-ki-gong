# ElderFund

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/ELDERFUND*

Discretionary government assistance scheme to support care needs of lower-income Singapore Citizens with severe disability.

## Highlights
- Up to $250 cash per month
- Processing time will take between 6 to 8 weeks
- Apply through AIC eServices for Financing Schemes (eFASS)

**Who it targets:** Low-income, Persons with disabilities
**Keywords:** Cash assistance, Lower-income, PWDs

## About this support
- Supports lower-income, severely disabled Singapore Citizens
- Provides up to $250 cash payout per month for as long as they remain eligible
- Payout can be used for long-term care needs such as hiring a helper and daily expenses

## Who is this for?
- Singapore Citizen aged 30 and above
- Lives in Singapore
- Has a monthly household income per capita of $1,500 or less
- Has a MediSave balance of less than $10,000
- Assessed by an MOH-accredited assessor to require full assistance with at least 3 out of the following 6 Activities of Daily Living (ADLs):
  - Washing
  - Dressing
  - Feeding
  - Toileting
  - Walking or moving around
  - Transferring
- Not a CareShield Life or ElderShield policyholder
- Not a CareShield Life or ElderShield supplement plan policyholder
- No longer an active recipient of ElderShield claims (including supplement claims)
- Not eligible for IDAPE, or no longer an active recipient of IDAPE

## How to apply?
You can apply:

- Online at the [AIC eServices for Financing Schemes (eFASS)](https://efinance.aic.sg/)
- Via assistance from the nursing home your loved one is staying in
- At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)

You will need to contact an MOH-accredited severe disability assessor for a Severe Disability Assessment (SDA) as part of the application. Visit the [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/Severe-Disability-Assessment) for a list of assessors.

Applications from Singaporeans who do not meet the guiding financial criteria but have difficulty meeting their long-term care needs will be considered on a case-by-case basis.
