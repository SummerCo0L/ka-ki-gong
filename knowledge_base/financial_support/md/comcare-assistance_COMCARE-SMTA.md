# ComCare Assistance

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/COMCARE-SMTA*

Provides financial support for individuals and families facing varied challenges, ensuring timely aid for both short-term and long-term needs.

## Highlights
- Cash assistance for lower-income individuals or families
- [If relevant] Assistance for household and medical expenses
- [If relevant] Provides other support such as employment assistance or social support services

**Who it targets:** Low-income, Vulnerable
**Keywords:** Cash assistance, Lower-income, Short-to-medium term

## About this support
**Interim Assistance**

Provides immediate financial assistance for lower-income individuals or families who require urgent and temporary financial relief. Assistance provided will depend on the household situation.

Interim Assistance will be enhanced and administered by MSF and the People’s Association. Applications can be made from 17 August to 31 December 2026.

**Short-to-Medium-Term Assistance**

Provides temporary financial assistance for lower-income individuals or families who are temporarily unable to work, looking for a job or earning a low income and require assistance.

- Monthly cash assistance for living expenses
- Assistance with your household bills, i.e. rental, utilities, and/or service and conservancy charges
- Medical assistance​ at public healthcare institutions
- Employment assistance such as job search and/or training
- Referrals for other relevant services

**Long-Term Assistance**

Provides long-term support to those permanently unable to work due to old age, illness or disability, and have inadequate family support or savings to rely on for daily needs.

- Monthly cash assistance for living expenses
- Assistance with medical expenses at public healthcare institutions
- Free or highly subsidised access to social support services

## Who is this for?
- You must be a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident (at least one member in the same household must be a Singapore Citizen).
- Households with a monthly household income per capita of $800 and below.

**Interim Assistance**

- Greater flexibility will be exercised in assessing income eligibility.

**Short-to-Medium-Term Assistance**

- Have little or no family support, savings or assets to rely on for your daily needs.
- Looking for work or temporarily unable to work due to illness or needing to care for children, elderly or other dependants.
- Even if your income exceeds these guidelines, you can still approach a Social Service Office (SSO) for assessment if you face financial difficulties. The SSOs will assess each household’s needs, such as employment, family and finances, to ensure that the assistance we provide is holistic.

**Long-Term Assistance**

- Unable to work because of old age, illness or disability.
- Elderly persons who receive a small monthly payout from sources such as CPF Retirement Sum / CPF Life.
- Elderly persons whose children are low income themselves i.e. monthly household income per capita of $800 and below.

## How to apply?
**Interim Assistance**

To apply:

- Online via [SupportGoWhere](https://supportgowhere.gov.sg/grants/smta)
- Alternatively, you may approach the following Community Partners :
  - [Social Service Offices](https://www.msf.gov.sg/our-services/directories#socialtab)
  - Community Clubs / Centres

**Short-to-Medium-Term Assistance / Long-Term Assistance**

Watch this [ComCare Financial Assistance Application Instructions video ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fRiYpEAhIhw)which provides applicants with information on the requirements of the ComCare application process and applicants' responsibilities while on assistance.

To apply:

- Online via [SupportGoWhere](https://supportgowhere.gov.sg/grants/smta)
- Alternatively, visit your [nearest Social Service Office](https://www.msf.gov.sg/our-services/directories#socialtab) to apply in person.

Each SSO administers ComCare financial assistance and helps applicants to access other forms of assistance they may need, such as employment assistance or family services.

You'll need to provide:

- Updated bank account passbooks / account summary / bank balance enquiry slips or similar for **all members**, clearly showing the latest bank balance in the last month
- Other supporting documents, **if relevant**:
  - Your identity card or birth certificate for **all members**
(Note: NRIC/FIN of main applicants will not be required for online applications as the login will be via Singpass. If applicant / household / other family member had provided these identification documents in earlier ComCare applications, they will not be required again.)
  - Latest utility bill(s) (e.g. Singapore Power (SP), private electricity retailers)
  - Latest Medical Certificate (with duration and fitness for work status)
  - Pay-slips / Employment contract (for employment without CPF contributions)
  - Latest employment / termination letter (for recent changes in employment status)
  - Insurance Letter (for lump-sum / regular payouts by private insurer)
  - Any other supporting documents

The collection, use or disclosure of personal data of relevant individuals was applied in the development of policies and services for lower-income households. In addition to the Personal Data Protection Act, there exists strict policies, processes and safeguards in place to ensure the proper use and protection of personal data by the Government.
