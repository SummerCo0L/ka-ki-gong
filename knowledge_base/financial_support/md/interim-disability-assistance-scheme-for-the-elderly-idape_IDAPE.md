# Interim Disability Assistance Scheme for the Elderly (IDAPE)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/IDAPE*

Provides monthly cash payout for elderly Singapore Citizens with severe disabilities who are not eligible for ElderShield.

## Highlights
- Receive $150 or $250 monthly cash payout for up to 72 months
- Processing time take between 6 to 8 weeks
- Apply through AIC’s eService for Financing Schemes (eFASS)

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities, Seniors
**Keywords:** Cash assistance, PWDs, Seniors

## About this support
Supports severely disabled seniors who are not eligible for ElderShield due to age or pre-existing disabilities, with monthly cash payouts of $150 or $250 for up to 72 months.

## Who is this for?
- Born on or before 30 September 1932 or
- Born between 1 October 1932 and 30 September 1962 (both dates inclusive), with pre-existing disabilities as of 30 September 2002
- Singapore Citizen living in Singapore
- Monthly household income per person of $3,600 or less (annual value of property under $21,00 for households with no income)
- Monthly payout depends on the monthly household income per person:
  Monthly Household Income Per Person
  Payout Amount
  $0-$2,600
  $250
  $2,601 - $3,600
  $150
- Not be a [CareShield Life](https://www.aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/CareShield-Life) or [ElderShield](https://www.aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/ElderShield) policyholder/ supplement plan policyholder, or have made any claims before

## How to apply?
You can apply:

- Online at the [AIC eServices for Financing Schemes (eFASS)](https://efinance.aic.sg/)
- Via assistance from the nursing home your loved one is staying in
- At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)

You will need to contact an MOH-accredited severe disability assessor for a Severe Disability Assessment (SDA) as part of the application. Visit the [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/Severe-Disability-Assessment) for a list of assessors.
