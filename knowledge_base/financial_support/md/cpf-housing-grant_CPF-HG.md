# CPF Housing Grant

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/CPF-HG*

Provide more support for first-time homebuyers to purchase resale HDB flats.

## Highlights
- Up to $80,000 for eligible first-time homebuyers purchasing resale HDB flats
- Increased by $30,000 for eligible First-Timer families purchasing 2-room to 4-room resale HDB flats
- Increased by $10,000 for eligible First-Timer families purchasing 5-room or larger resale HDB flats

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Keywords:** Budget 2023, Families, Housing grants

## About this support
**Eligible First-Timer families**

- Qualify for $80,000 grant when purchasing 2-room to 4-room resale flats
- Qualify for $50,000 grant when purchasing 5-room or larger resale flats

**Eligible First-Timer singles**

- Qualify for $40,000 grant when purchasing 2-room to 4-room resale flats
- Qualify for $25,000 grant when purchasing 5-room resale flats

## Who is this for?
- Applicant must be a Singapore Citizen (SC)
- At least 1 applicant must be a first-timer i.e. have not received any housing subsidy before
- Average gross monthly household income must not exceed:
  - $16,000 for families
  - $24,000 for extended families
  - $8,000 for singles purchasing a flat on their own
- All applicants and occupiers cannot own or have an interest in a private property in the last 30 months prior to flat application
