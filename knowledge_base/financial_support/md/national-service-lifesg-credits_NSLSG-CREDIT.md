# National Service LifeSG Credits

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/NSLSG-CREDIT*

$200 in LifeSG credits for all eligible past and present national servicemen to recognise their contributions to Singapore’s defence and security.

## Highlights
- $200 in LifeSG credits
- For all eligible past and present national servicemen
- Credits have been automatically disbursed to eligible national servicemen in 2024
- Valid for 1 year from the date of disbursement

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Keywords:** Budget 2024, NSFs, NSmen

## About this support
- $200 in LifeSG credits for all eligible past and present national servicemen, accessible via the ‘Your Benefits’ section of the [LifeSG mobile application](https://go.life.gov.sg/5yCp).
- The credits are valid for 1 year from the date of disbursement. The validity period will not be extended.
- Eligible national servicemen may check on the validity period of their credits through the LifeSG mobile application.

**Where to use the NS LifeSG Credits:**

- The credits can be spent at online or physical merchants accepting payments via PayNow UEN QR or NETS QR.
- As a show of support for National Service, several merchants are offering additional discounts and promotions when payments are made using the NS LifeSG Credits. For the list of participating merchants, please refer [here.](https://go.gov.sg/promosnslifesg)

## Who is this for?
- Full-Time National Servicemen (NSFs) who have enlisted by 31 Dec 2024.
- Past and present Operationally Ready National Servicemen (NSmen).
- Disbursements are subject to further eligibility checks.
- Eligible national servicemen were notified by SMS and letter in 2024.

## How to apply?
- No application required.
- The credits have been automatically disbursed via the LifeSG mobile application to eligible past and present national servicemen in 2024.
- An SMS notification and notification letter were sent to eligible national servicemen after the credits were disbursed.

## Where to go
Walk in to any [SG Digital Community Hub](http://go.gov.sg/sdch-nslifesgcredits2024) or [ServiceSG Centre](https://www.psd.gov.sg/servicesg/) if you face difficulty in redeeming your credits.
