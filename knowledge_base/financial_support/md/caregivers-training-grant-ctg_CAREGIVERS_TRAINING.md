# Caregivers Training Grant (CTG)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/CAREGIVERS_TRAINING*

Subsidy for caregivers to offset the training fees of approved courses to equip themselves with the necessary skills to care for their loved ones.

## Highlights
- $400 subsidy for the first year
- $200 annual top-up, with a total CTG balance cap of $400 at any given time
- Contact the service provider at least two weeks before the course starts

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities, Seniors
**Keywords:** Caregiver support, Subsidies, Trainings

## About this support
- $400 grant for eligible care recipients in their first year of tapping on CTG
- CTG balance can be used to subsidise their caregivers attending approved caregiver training courses
- Co-payment of at least $10 for every course attended by the caregiver
- $200 annual top-up in subsequent years, with total CTG balance capped at $400 at any given time
- If multiple caregivers of the same care recipient wish to attend training, they can tap on the same CTG balance available to offset the course fees

## Who is this for?
Care recipient must be:

- Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- Aged 65 and older

OR

- Have a disability assessed by an approved healthcare practitioner

Caregiver must be:

- Main person caring for the care recipient (e.g. family members and foreign domestic workers)

## How to apply?
- [Choose an approved course here](https://training-healthcare.vertis.digital/)
- Contact the training provider to confirm course details
- Register for the course
- Inform the trainer about using CTG at least two weeks before the course starts
- Your trainer will give you the CTG application form to fill out, and will submit it on your behalf
- [You can also visit the AIC’s website to download the CTG application form](https://www.aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/Caregivers-Training-Grant)
