# Home Caregiving Grant (HCG)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/HCG*

Provides financial support to help with caregiving expenses for households caring for persons with moderate to severe disabilities.

## Highlights
- Payouts of $200, $400 or $600 per month
- Processing time takes up to 4 weeks
- Apply through AIC’s eService portal (eFASS)

**Who it targets:** Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors
**Keywords:** Caregiving expenses, Cash assistance, PWDs

## About this support
Monthly cash payout of $200, $400 or $600

## Who is this for?
Care recipient must meet the following criteria:

- Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident with a parent, child or spouse who is a Singapore Citizen
- Lives in Singapore
- Always requires some assistance on a permanent basis with at least 3 of the 6 Activities of Daily Living (ADLs)
- Monthly per capita household income of less than or equal to $4,800 or less  (or property annual value of $21,000 or below for households with no income)
- Those with multiple properties will receive the lowest HCG payout of $200 per month
- [Not in a residential long-term care institution](https://www.aic.sg/financial-assistance/home-caregiving-grant#q5a)

The 6 ADLs are:

- Eating
- Bathing
- Dressing
- Transferring
- Toileting
- Walking or moving around

## How to apply?
You can apply:

- Online at the [AIC eService Portal](https://efinance.aic.sg/)
- At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us)

You'll need to provide:

- [Completed Functional Assessment Report](https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/agencyforinb6cc-agencyforin73f5-production08ac-d178/media/agency-for-integrated-care/Files/Financial-Assistance/Functional-Assessment-Report.pdf) from your General Practitioner, a registered Nurse or a registered Physio/Occupational Therapist
- NRIC details
- Bank details
