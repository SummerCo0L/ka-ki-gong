# YMCA Vocational and Soft Skills Programme (VaSSP)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SVC-YVASSPV*

Aims to equip out-of-school youths and youths-at-risk with job competency as well as specific vocational skills.

**Who it targets:** Vulnerable
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Referral required, Youths at risk

## About this support
The YMCA Vocational and Soft Skills Programme (VaSSP), also known as Project Bridge, aims to equip out-of-school youths and youths-at-risk of dropping out (who are absent from school for a long period) with essential soft skills and vocational expertise, enabling them to enhance their employability to integrate into the workforce or to reintegrate to education.

Conducted over 6 months, the programme consists to three phases: Core Training, Vocational Training, and Monitoring Phase. Service users engage in soft skills training, covering crucial areas such as goal setting, resume writing, interview skills, and communication and conflict resolution.

Subsequently, youths can select one out of 14 vocational tracks and receive certified training in the chosen industry. Thereafter, YMCA staff will continue to monitor the service users for a period of time before completion of the programme by supporting them in aspects such as career counselling or academic support in the service users' journey to either obtain employment or reintegrate back into education.

## Who is this for?
- Unemployed youths or school leavers between 15 and 21 years old
- Educational attainment of 'N' levels or below
- Pass the admission interview
- Able to commit and attend full-time training, and achieve 75% attendance and above

## How to apply?
- Email - projectbridge@ymca.org.sg
- Students can be referred by school counsellor or other social service agencies

## What to expect?
- Interested parties can write in to VaSSP and a staff will be in touch within 3 working days
- Youths will need to go through admission interview and meet the criteria of the programme
