# MyCareersFuture 

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/tmyOMyke*

The one-stop portal for job search and application needs. It uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) to help jobseekers discover career opportunities, make career transitions, or search for jobs based on skills matches.  It also provides valuable career-related resources, including career insights and career guidance to facilitate their career planning and career development processes.

## Highlights
- Our latest Job-to-Skills job matching algorithm connects you with opportunities tailored to your skills and experience.
- Utilise CareersFinder to discover personalised upskilling and career recommendations based on your unique profile.
- Filter jobs eligible for Government support such as Career Conversion Programmes, Graduate Industry Traineeships (GRIT) Programme and Mid-Career Pathways Programmes
- Elevate your skills with our curated articles, webinars, and courses designed for your professional growth.

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Keywords:** Employment Support and Vocational Training, Jobseekers

## About this support
- **Advanced Matching Algorithm: **Our latest Job-to-Skills job matching algorithm connects you with opportunities tailored to your skills and experience.
- **CareersFinder by MyCareersFuture: **[CareersFinder](https://careersfinder.mycareersfuture.gov.sg/) harnesses Government data and artificial intelligence to help individuals discover personalised upskilling and career recommendations based on their unique profile to achieve their career goals.
- **Government Support Eligibility: **Filter jobs eligible for Government support such as:
  - Career Conversion Programmes (CCP) if you are open to reskill and change career/occupations
  - GRaduate Industry and Traineeship Programme (GRIT) if you are a fresh graduate looking for a traineeship opportunity, and
  - Mid-Career Pathways Programmes (MCPP) if you are a mature individual looking for a mid-career attachments.
- **CareersCompass by MyCareersFuture:** Access curated articles, career guides and expert insights to help you navigate career planning, skills development and job search strategies.
- **CareersHorizon by MyCareersFuture: **A one-stop digital platform for career-related event registrations and management. It enables Singaporeans to access a comprehensive suite of career resources, including online articles, virtual career events, seminars, and workshops. The platform also offers micro-learning content, such as podcasts, covering topics relevant to all career stages. Additionally, it supports Singaporeans in searching and registering for career events, and it also facilitates networking opportunities between employers and individuals.

## Who is this for?
Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents who are looking to plan their careers, make career transitions, or search for jobs.

## How to apply?
Embark on your journey to professional success with [MyCareersFuture](https://www.mycareersfuture.gov.sg/).
