# Women at Work (W@W)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/WJtDbT4F*

The W@W programme is designed to support Malay/Muslim women to re-enter the workforce.

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Citizenship:** SG, PR
**Keywords:** Employment Support and Vocational Training, Free

## About this support
The W@W programme is designed to support Malay/Muslim women who have been unemployed for more than 6 months and seeking to re-enter the workforce.

## Who is this for?
Malay/Muslim women who have been unemployed for more than 6 months and seeking to re-enter the workforce.
