# Enabling Business Hub

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/LPsRp3sD*

Developed by SG Enable, Enabling Business Hubs (EBHs) bring training and employment opportunities closer to homes for persons with disabilities through customised job support and inclusive workplace practices.

## Highlights
- Employment for PwDs
- Training and employment opportunities for PwDs

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities
**Keywords:** Employment Support and Vocational Training, Jobseekers, PWDs

## About this support
With an on-site Employment Support Team, persons with disabilities can look forward to a comprehensive suite of employment-related services such as vocational assessment, training, job-matching and post-placement job support. These services aim to better match persons with disabilities to jobs in the region based on their skills and interests and support them in employment.

EBH@Jurong

Located beside Lakeside MRT, at 111A Yuan Ching Road, Singapore 619606, EBH@Jurong aims to bring training and employment opportunities closer to the homes of persons with disabilities living in the West, including those with higher work support needs.

Key Features of EBH@Jurong

1. Provide targeted training and support** **to prepare persons with disabilities for employment in the logistics sector.
2. Provide on-site employment support, including longer and more intensive employment support for persons with disabilities with higher work support needs and require a longer runway of support to work independently in the community.

## Who is this for?
Applicants should be:

- Aged 16 and above
- Singapore Citizens or Permanent Residents
- Verified to have a permanent disability of any of the following:
  - Physical Disability: Requires some assistance with at least one of the six Activities of Daily Living due to physical impairment
  - Moderate visual impairment or worse in the better eye
  - Moderate hearing loss or worse in the better ear
  - Intellectual disability
  - Autism
- Preferably living in the West or keen to work in the Logistics industry

## How to apply?
**Prior to application, applicants are encouraged to check if your disability status has already been verified by logging into **[**SupportGoWhere**](https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/status/pwdr)**. **

**Preparation of documents**

The following supporting documents are required before making an application:

- [Disability Verification Form (DVF)](https://www.enablingguide.sg/docs/default-source/default-document-library/disability-verification-form.pdf) (To be completed and endorsed by registered healthcare professional if disability status is unverified)
- Copy of NRIC Front and Back
- [One Referral Form](https://www.enablingguide.sg/docs/default-source/default-document-library/one-referral-form-with-omnibus_2025.pdf?sfvrsn=ab89bee_1) (For email/physical submissions only)

**How to apply**

Persons with disabilities seeking employment assistance may:

1. Apply online via [SupportGoWhere](https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/grants/ebh/apply),[ ](#_msocom_5)or
2. Download the One Referral Form (ORF) and email the supporting documents to EBH@Jurong at [EBH_Jurong@bizlink.org.sg](mailto:EBH_Jurong@bizlink.org.sg) or;
3. Contact [EBH_Jurong@bizlink.org.sg](mailto:EBH_Jurong@bizlink.org.sg) to schedule an appointment with the EBH@Jurong employment services team.

Persons with disabilities keen on other employment programmes, please email us at [employment@sgenable.sg](mailto:employment@sgenable.sg).
