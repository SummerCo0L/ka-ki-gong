# Career Conversion Programme (CCP) for Individuals

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/CCPIND*

Support mid-career new hires or existing employees seeking to transition into growth job roles with good longer-term prospects.

## Highlights
- Undergo on-the-job training to take up new growth job roles
- Salary support of up to 90% for mid-career individuals
- Apply through CCP-supportable jobs on MyCareersFuture

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Keywords:** Employment Support and Vocational Training, Mid-career, Workers

## About this support
- Access on-the-job training to switch careers into growth sectors with long-term prospects
- Salary support of up to 90% for mid-career workers
- Embark on a new career with CCPs across around 30 sectors

## Who is this for?
Mid-career individuals should meet the following criteria:

- Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- Aged 21 or above
- Graduated or completed National Service, for at least 2 years
- Career conversion is required:
  - New Hire: New job role trained for under the CCP should be substantially different from previous job role before joining CCP
  - Existing Employee: New job role should be growth job roles identified under the Jobs Transformation Maps (JTMs)
- Able to commit to full-time employment or attachment in a participating company

Beyond the general eligibility criteria, different CCPs may have additional course-specific entry requirements, and employers may apply their own -specific shortlisting process. In general, individuals applying for CCPs should also not be shareholders, immediate ex-staff, or related to owners/decision-makers of the CCP participating company or its related entities.

## How to apply?
Apply for CCPs here through CCP-supportable jobs on [MyCareersFuture](https://www.mycareersfuture.gov.sg/).
