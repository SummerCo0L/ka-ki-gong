# SkillsFuture Series

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/SFSERIES*

Provides short, industry-relevant training for in-demand skills in Care, Digital, Green, and Industry 4.0 sectors.

## Highlights
- Offers short, industry-relevant training programmes
- Focus on emerging skills in Care, Digital, Green, and Industry 4.0 sectors
- Training across Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced levels for different proficiencies

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Keywords:** Courses, Employment Support and Vocational Training, Trainings

## About this support
- Training across Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced levels to match different skill proficiencies
- Acquire foundational or advanced skills to boost career prospects and enhance employability

## Who is this for?
- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents, criteria may vary depending on courses

## How to apply?
To enrol in a SkillsFuture Series course:

- Visit the [MySkillsFuture portal](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/index.html)
- Log in with your Singpass to access the course directory
- Search for SkillsFuture Series courses under the respective growth areas: [Care Economy](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/training-exchange/course-directory.html?fq=Course_Supp_Period_To_1:[2022-12-15T00:00:00Z TO *]&fq=IsValid:true&q=care economy), [Digital Economy](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/training-exchange/course-directory.html?fq=Course_Supp_Period_To_1:[2022-12-15T00:00:00Z TO *]&fq=IsValid:true&q=digital economy), [Green Economy](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/training-exchange/course-directory.html?fq=Course_Supp_Period_To_1:[2022-12-15T00:00:00Z TO *]&fq=IsValid:true&q=green economyI), or [Industry 4.0](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/training-exchange/course-directory.html?fq=Course_Supp_Period_To_1:[2022-12-15T00:00:00Z TO *]&fq=IsValid:true&q=industry 4.0)
- Select a course at the Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced level according to your proficiency
- Follow the instructions to sign up for your chosen course
