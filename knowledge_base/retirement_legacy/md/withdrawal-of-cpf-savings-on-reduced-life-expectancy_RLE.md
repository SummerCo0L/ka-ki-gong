# Withdrawal of CPF Savings on Reduced Life Expectancy

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/RLE*

Members with reduced life expectancy due to a medical condition, as certified by an accredited doctor (from public hospitals, polyclinics, or a specialist), may apply to withdraw some of their CPF savings. Severe medical conditions causing them to be permanently unfit for work or to lack mental capacity permanently can also be considered.

## Highlights
- Withdraw $5,000 or more, depending on extent of your reduced life expectancy 
- Lump sum as well as monthly payouts from your Retirement Account, if applicable
- Apply via CPF e-service and approach the Medical Institution to complete the Medical Assessment Report 

**Who it targets:** Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Keywords:** Cash assistance, CPF payouts, PWDs

## About this support
- Depending on the extent of your reduced life expectancy and your CPF balances, you may withdraw a lump sum of $5,000^ or more
- You may also need to set aside a reduced retirement sum in your Retirement Account (RA), from which you will receive monthly payouts

^ Excludes monies received under the Retirement Sum Topping-Up Scheme and Matched Retirement Savings Scheme, government grants and your MediSave Account savings.

## Who is this for?
Singapore Citizen (SC) and Permanent Resident (PR) who have any of the following conditions as certified by an accredited doctor:

- Severely reduced life expectancy due to medical condition
- Permanently unfit for work
- Permanently lack mental capacity to make decisions, as defined in the Mental Capacity Act

## How to apply?
**Applying for self**

- Submit [an online application](https://cpf.gov.sg/member/tools-and-services/forms-e-applications/withdraw-cpf-due-to-reduced-life-expectancy-and-claim-insurance) after logging in with your Singpass

- Download the form “Medical Assessment Report” that is provided upon submission of the online application and have it completed by an [accredited doctor](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/who-is-considered-an-accredited-doctor). Thereafter, please request the medical institution to submit the completed medical assessment report to CPF Board directly (instructions are provided within the form).
- If you are mailing your documents from overseas, all documents must be witnessed/certified true by an official of a Singapore Overseas Mission with his official seal/stamp duly affixed.

**Applying as a donee or deputy for your next-of-kin**

Submit [this form](http://cpf.gov.sg/applyrlehpsdps) with a copy of your NRIC attached and provide the following original documents to CPF Board:

- Medical report from an [accredited doctor](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/who-is-considered-an-accredited-doctor) stating that the member lacks mental capacity permanently;

- Lasting Power of Attorney or Court Order; and

- Statement for the bank account held on trust on behalf of the member.

Make [an appointment](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/contact-us/visit-us) to visit any of our CPF Service Centres to submit the above original documents or mail them to:

CPF Board 
Retirement Withdrawals Department (RWD) 
Privy Box No. 920651

Singapore 929292
