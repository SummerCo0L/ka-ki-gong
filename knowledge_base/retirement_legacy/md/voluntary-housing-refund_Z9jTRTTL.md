# Voluntary Housing Refund 

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/Z9jTRTTL*

Allows you to return any CPF savings used for your home purchase to your CPF account while still owning your property. Doing so reduces the refund amount you would have to make and increases the cash proceeds you would get when you eventually sell or transfer your property. This also boosts your retirement savings as you can benefit from CPF interest rates.

## Highlights
- Grow your retirement savings with CPF interest rates
- Greater cash proceeds and lower required CPF refund upon property sale or transfer

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Keywords:** CPF top-ups

## About this support
- Receive more cash proceeds and refund less to your CPF account when selling or transferring your property
- Take advantage of CPF’s interest rates and retire with more
- Re-use CPF savings for the various CPF approved schemes if needed

## Who is this for?
- You have used your Ordinary Account (OA) savings to purchase your property
- Please note the following conditions when making the voluntary housing refund:
  - You can refund up to the CPF principal amount withdrawn towards the property plus its accrued interest to your CPF account
  - If you are an undischarged bankrupt, you have to obtain the Official Assignee’s approval to make this housing refund
  - While housing grants will generally be refunded to your OA if you have an HDB flat, the grant(s) may be partially credited to your Special Account / Retirement Account and MediSave Account if you have received more than $30,000 in grant(s)
  - If you are 55 and above, the refunded amount [will be used to meet your Full Retirement Sum (FRS)](http://www.cpf.gov.sg/VHRAge55), which supports your retirement needs through monthly payouts
  - Any refunds beyond your cohort’s FRS will remain in your OA
- You can read more on [housing refunds and their processes](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/infohub/educational-resources/sales-proceeds-after-selling-your-home)

## How to apply?
Check the amount of voluntary refund you can make under the “What happens if” section in your [Home ownership dashboard](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/homeownershipdashboard).

You can make the voluntary housing refund through the following methods:

- Log in to [my cpf digital services](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/tools-and-services/forms-e-applications/make-a-voluntary-housing-refund) using your Singpass:
  - Choose your property and enter a refund amount

Or

- Use the CPF Mobile app:
  - Download the CPF Mobile app
  - Log in with your Singpass
  - Tap on 'Services'
  - Choose 'Voluntary Housing Refund'
