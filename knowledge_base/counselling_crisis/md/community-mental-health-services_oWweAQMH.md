# Community Mental Health Services

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/oWweAQMH*

Community mental health teams located across Singapore provide support and access to mental health care for those in need.

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Counselling, Intervention and Therapy, Social-emotional Support

## About this support
Mental health support services are offered by over 100 community mental health teams islandwide, with at least 1 team in every neighbourhood. These teams serve as the first stop for in-person mental health support.

## Who is this for?
Anyone aged 12 and above, and in need of mental health support.

## How to apply?
- Visit the [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Community-Mental-Health-Services) to search for service providers by** **postal code, address or your device’s current location.
- Contact the team directly for an appointment.

For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline[1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).

- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.
- Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.

## What to expect?
- Every visit is strictly confidential.
- Trained care professionals in each team will help identify your needs, develop a personalised care plan and provide support such as counselling and therapy.
- They can also help to escalate and make necessary referrals if needed.
