# Live On!

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SVC-LOBTCS*

Year-long, family-centric psychological intervention for youths with high suicide risk as they transit from hospitalisation into the community.

**Who it targets:** Families, Vulnerable
**Citizenship:** SG, PR
**Keywords:** Free, Referral required, Suicide Intervention

## About this support
- LiveOn! is a 1-year high frequency and intensive suicide intervention programme that targets youths aged 12 to 18 years old with suicidality risk, and includes their family members in the intervention.
- Objectives of the programme includes:
  - Providing immediate support to youths with suicidal thoughts or attempts in the community
  - Providing continuous community support for youths and the families once they are discharged from primary care institutions
  - De-escalating youths' emotions and strengthening family support through individual and family counselling

## Who is this for?
- 12 to 18 years old youths who have been discharged from IMH due to suicidality.

## How to apply?
- This programme is only eligible for youths who have been discharged by IMH, KKH, NUH, and other public hospitals due to suicidality, and have been assessed by a clinician to be eligible for referral.
- Call the TOUCHline (1800 377 2252, 9am to 6pm from Monday to Friday) or email [mentalhealth@touch.org.sg](mailto:mentalhealth@touch.org.sg) if you do not have a hospital referral but still require support.
- If you are suicidal and require immediate support, call Samaritans of Singapore (SOS) at 1767.

## What to expect?
This programme consists of individual counselling for the youths, as well as family sessions.

**Individual sessions (for the youth)**

- In the Acute phase, the youth will meet with the social worker / counsellor / psychologist weekly for 6 to 12 sessions to de-escalate emotions.
- In the Continuation phase, the youth will meet with the social worker / counsellor / psychologist twice a month, for another 5 to 12 sessions.
- In the Maintenance phase after the above is completed, the youth will remain in contact with the caseworker through calls and text messages for 3 to 6 months for continuous support and monitoring . If youths relapse, an assessment will be conducted and the case will be re-opened.

**Family sessions (for the caregivers of the youth)**

- Meet with the social worker / counsellor twice a month from the start of the programme for 4 to 6 sessions to learn skills on how to support the youth better, which includes safety planning and crisis management School counsellors will also be contacted, with client and parental consent.
