# Maintenance Support Central (MSC)

*Source: https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/SVC-MSCM*

Provides support and assistance for clients who have difficulty in receiving spousal and/or child maintenance.

**Who it targets:** Families, Low-income, Persons with disabilities, Seniors, Vulnerable
**Citizenship:** SG, PR, OTHERS
**Keywords:** Free

## About this support
MSC empowers individuals by providing knowledge of their marital and maintenance rights while offering comprehensive support in collaboration with community partners.

1.      Maintenance Applications

- Assists in the filing of Fresh Maintenance Applications (FMO) via the Integrated Family Application Management System (iFAMS)
- Assists in the Enforcement of Maintenance Orders (EMO) through video-link filing (iFAMS)
- Appointments must be made in advance

2.      Legal Clinics

- Offers Civil Family Law legal clinic on the first and third Monday evenings of each month
- Offers Syariah Family Law legal clinic on the third Monday of each month
- Legal clinics are open to individuals residing in Singapore, with an income cap criteria

3.      Court Process Guidance

- Offers legal guidance which includes explanation of standard legal procedures and legal terms related to family law

4.      Counselling Services

- Provides face-to-face and phone counselling for individuals who require emotional support for marital issues, post-divorce/separations, parenthood struggles, and/or similar concerns
- Counselling services are chargeable, based on clients’ financial capabilities

5.      Educational Talks

- Legal talks on family law matters designed to benefit the public, social service professionals, and community partners. These sessions aim to raise awareness, enhance understanding, and equip participants with practical knowledge to navigate legal challenges effectively.
- Talks are conducted by guest speakers who are experts in Family and Syariah law

MSC also assists in responding to enquiries by sharing information or referring them to other community partners.

## Who is this for?
- Services are rendered to individuals residing in Singapore who face financial abuse, marital issues, or have a Singapore court order and require legal support to navigate family law matters.
- This includes individuals who are dealing with unpaid maintenance, divorce proceedings, and financial difficulties arising from marital conflicts.

## How to apply?
- You may apply to file Fresh or Enforcement of Maintenance application online (https://ifams.gov.sg/sop/#iFAMS). Alternatively, you may also contact the MSC programme's hotline at 6837 0611 to make an appointment.
- You can register for the online legal clinic (https://forms.office.com/r/4JucwmuQfc). If you qualify, programme staff will get back to you by 3 working days on the details.
- You may register for counselling service online (https://forms.office.com/r/mvdxTJcBFF) or call the hotline at 6571 0185 / 6837 0611 (WhatsApp)

## What to expect?
Service users who require the programme’s services will need to complete a registration form. Staff will confirm the appointments with the service user within 3 to 5 working days of form submission. For clients who register for legal clinic or counselling service, the processing time is 3 working days. All other services can be arranged at the time of contact.
